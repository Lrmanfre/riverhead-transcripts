January 30, 2025 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting

Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board work session covered a $1.4 million state grant for the downtown amphitheater, plans for a new Riverhead Volunteer Ambulance Corps headquarters, proposed updates to town board rules, beach sticker policy for first responders, new fire marshal fees for battery energy storage and solar installations, and a batch of routine resolutions.

Key actions

  • The board advanced plans to issue an RFP for a new approximately 16,600-square-foot RVAC ambulance headquarters to replace the 1989 building, with financing expected through a combination of transport billing revenue, fundraising, and a town bond.
  • The board discussed codifying the annual free beach and 4x4 sticker benefit for active volunteer firefighters and EMTs rather than reauthorizing it each year by resolution.
  • The Fire Marshal presented a proposal to add new construction and annual operating permit fees specifically for battery energy storage and solar installations, modeled on Brookhaven's approach.
  • The board approved approximately 32 resolutions including personnel appointments, retirements, bid awards, grant submissions, and a public hearing on electrical upgrades at Water District Plant No. 7 set for February 13.
  • A public hearing on proposed amendments to the town's Excavation and Grading fee schedule was scheduled for March 4, 2025 at 2 p.m.
  • The board entered executive session to discuss personnel matters, a PBA and SOA contract, sale of real property, and an environmental facilities contractual agreement.

Money

  • The town's Community Development Agency was awarded $1.4 million through New York State Empire State Development's Pro Housing Program for the downtown amphitheater project.
  • A separate award of approximately $187,000 was received for water infrastructure supporting the Suffolk Theater.
  • RVAC reported collecting roughly $100,000 in transport billing revenue in January 2025 alone, with an estimated potential of up to $1 million per year to help fund the new building.

Discussed

  • The town attorney presented a draft update to the town board's rules and procedures, including a proposed three-minute public comment limit, a sign-in sheet system for speakers, a ban on signs inside the board chamber, expanded decorum rules, and a requirement that local laws not be adopted by ratification.
  • The board discussed attaching an annual CPI adjustment to fire marshal and other town fees so rates do not go decades without revision.
  • The possibility of allowing town employees who live outside Riverhead to purchase beach stickers was raised but set aside due to a legal concern about creating unequal employee benefits; the idea was referred to the beach committee for further review.
