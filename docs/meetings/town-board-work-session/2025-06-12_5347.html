June 12, 2025 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Town Board work session covered a proposed no-parking code amendment on Youngs Avenue, closed out dozens of capital projects, and addressed an emergency liquid sludge removal contract, among other routine resolutions.

Key actions

  • The board discussed amending Chapter 289-10 to codify a no-parking restriction on both sides of Youngs Avenue between East Meadow Road and Osborne Avenue, with a public hearing to be scheduled for the next available meeting and possible same-day adoption.
  • A resolution was reviewed to declare an emergency and authorize a temporary purchasing agreement for liquid sludge removal and disposal to Suffolk County's Bergen Point facility while a permanent contract is arranged, expected within 30 to 60 days.
  • Resolutions were listed to close approximately two dozen capital projects across water, sewer, highway, and parks departments.
  • A resolution to rescind a prior sewer improvement bid (resolution 2025-487 for 203-213 East Main Street) was included as an administrative cleanup, with the bid to be re-issued after additional steps are completed.
  • A bid for lubricants was rejected due to a single unsatisfactory response, and the town clerk was authorized to rebid.
  • A parcel at 48 Willow Street is proposed to be designated open space using $75,000 in Community Preservation Funds.
  • The board authorized a fireworks application for Riverhead Raceway on June 28, 2025, and for Baiting Hollow Club on July 19, 2025.

Discussed

  • The supervisor announced the town received its first cannabis tax distribution from New York State totaling $65,000 for roughly the first quarter of sales.
  • Board members discussed establishing truck weight enforcement operations on roads near industrial properties on Youngs Avenue, where heavy trucks have been observed traveling on weight-restricted roads.
  • The board viewed a Riverhead in Action video recapping a farm tour of four local agricultural operations including cannabis, vertical farming, and greenhouse growing.
