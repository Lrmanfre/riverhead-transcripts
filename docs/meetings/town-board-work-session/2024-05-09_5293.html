May 9, 2024 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting

Summary AI

The Town Board held a work session to discuss a special permit application from Riverhead Charter School to place three temporary portable trailers at its 5117 Sound Avenue location. The board agreed to schedule a public hearing on the application.

Key actions

  • The board agreed to schedule a public hearing on the charter school's special permit application for three portable trailers at 5117 Sound Avenue.
  • The board moved into executive session to discuss possible changes in status for several employees and a possible food concession license agreement.

Discussed

  • Riverhead Charter School is seeking a special permit to add three temporary portable trailers behind its schoolhouse: two for classrooms and one for administration, to accommodate an increase in enrollment from 106 to 154 students.
  • One trailer was already brought to the site without prior approval; the school's representative apologized and the applicant noted the fee would be doubled as a result.
  • Board members expressed concern about the trailer being placed without permission and about the potential for a six-year 'temporary' situation becoming permanent.
  • The applicant stated the trailers would be leased year-to-year, fully screened from Sound Avenue, and removed when the school relocates, which the superintendent said was the school's firm intention.
  • The sanitary system was described as sized to handle the increased occupancy, and fire marshal access requirements can be met along the west side of the building.
  • Board members suggested the special permit could include an annual renewal requirement and a cap on further expansion at that site.
  • The school reported it capped kindergarten enrollment this year and is actively seeking a new permanent location, having lost a contract on an adjacent parcel that was never fully subdivided.
