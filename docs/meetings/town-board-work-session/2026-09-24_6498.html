September 24, 2026 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Town Board reviewed the 2027 tentative budget presentation, heard an update that a business advisory committee has not yet weighed in on proposed private special event legislation, and approved plans for a fall pumpkin-smashing environmental event, before moving into executive session on personnel, contractual, and legal matters.

Money

  • The 2027 tentative general fund budget stays within the state tax cap of 2.79% and increases total appropriations to about $116.9 million, roughly $4.1 million more than 2026.
  • For a property valued at $800,000, the tax increase is estimated at about 28 cents per day, and residential waste costs are expected to rise less than 22 cents per month.
  • The budget includes six new full-time positions (including a maintenance mechanic, an equipment operator, a building inspector, a purchasing clerk, a town clerk office assistant, and an additional fire marshal), along with nine promotional raises and 76 merit raises, some tied to added salary schedule steps in seniors, buildings and grounds, and highway departments.
  • Savings of about $819,000 in the general fund resulted from a 2026 retirement incentive taken by seven PBA and three CSEA members, offset partly by increased retiree health insurance costs.
  • The adult-use cannabis tax revenue projection was raised by $200,000 to $600,000, partly because Brookhaven now receives a share of the allocation.
  • About $1,500 in CDA funds is available to cover costs of the proposed pumpkin-smashing event.

Discussed

  • A council member reported that the Business Advisory Committee has not yet reviewed or voted on proposed private special event legislation and requested more time.
  • A board member presented a proposal for a pumpkin-smashing event at the yard waste facility to promote the food scraps composting program and boost Climate Smart points, tentatively set for Saturday, November 7, from about 11 a.m. to 3 or 4 p.m., the same day as a separate town cleanup event.

Affects residents

  • Fall cleanup, including a new 'plogging' litter-collection activity, is scheduled for Saturday, November 7, from 9 to 11 a.m. at Town Hall.
  • Trunk-or-treat registration begins September 23 through the Recreation Department for an event at Stotzky Park on October 16.
  • The Wading River Fall Festival, originally planned for this Sunday, has been rescheduled to Sunday, October 18 at the Shops at East Wind due to weather.
  • Residents were urged to monitor the town website for updates on expected weekend storms with strong winds, heavy rain, and possible coastal flooding, as highway crews prepare drainage and equipment.
