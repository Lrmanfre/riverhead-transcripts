June 27, 2024 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting

Summary AI

The board held a lengthy work session covering a $24 million federal downtown grant, detailed line-by-line review of the draft comprehensive plan, and previewed roughly 40 consent resolutions for upcoming action.

Key actions

  • The town received a $24.12 million federal Department of Transportation grant for downtown revitalization covering a parking garage, flood mitigation, and streetscaping.
  • The board reached consensus to review the 500-unit residential cap in the DC-1 zone annually rather than only after build-out.
  • The board agreed that condominium and ownership units should not count against the 500-unit cap, and that apartments in existing historic buildings would also be excluded.
  • The board decided to retain the 28-day minimum stay requirement for short-term rentals, with one dissenting member.
  • The board agreed to eliminate the three-year certificate-of-occupancy waiting period for accessory dwelling unit structures (the requirement for the principal structure may remain).
  • The board decided to strike charter schools as a permitted use in industrial zones while allowing assisted living to remain by special permit in any district.
  • The board directed that clarifying language be added to the plan and zoning code confirming that floor-area-ratio limits do not apply to renewable energy projects such as solar and battery storage.
  • A special permit application for a proposed 15,000-square-foot professional office building at 374 Main Road was advanced toward a planning board public hearing with a recommended negative declaration under state environmental review.
  • A bond resolution appropriating $2,260,000 was scheduled for re-adoption due to a prior vote lacking the required number of board members present.
  • Public hearing notices were authorized for proposed amendments to the zoning code covering bed-and-breakfast facilities, peddling and soliciting regulations, and noise/nuisance variance procedures.
  • The board approved fireworks applications for the Jamesport Fire Department on July 20, the Riverhead BID on July 5, and a local golf club on July 5.
