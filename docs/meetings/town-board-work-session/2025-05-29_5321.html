May 29, 2025 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting

Summary AI

The Town Board work session covered a promotional EMS Week video featuring Riverhead Volunteer Ambulance Corps, proposed zoning amendments along Route 58, a special permit application for a motocross track in Calverton, and a list of routine administrative resolutions. The board then moved into executive session.

Key actions

  • The board discussed amending the shopping center and business center zoning districts along Route 58 to permit additional uses, including retail, personal services, banks, and drive-through restaurants, consistent with the comprehensive plan.
  • Movie theaters are proposed to be moved from special permit use to permitted use in the shopping center zoning district after board members noted resident demand and a simpler approval process.
  • Five properties along Harrison Avenue, currently zoned shopping center, are recommended to be rezoned to commercial residential campus because of their proximity to single-family residences.
  • The board reviewed a special permit application for a motocross track at 2822 River Road, Calverton, on a 15-acre light industrial parcel; the planner will prepare resolutions to initiate SEQRA coordination and retain an independent noise engineer to review the applicant's noise study.
  • A new Garfield Langhorne Memorial monument was relocated to the front entrance of Town Hall, with Monument Works donating all labor at no cost.
  • The board approved a slate of roughly 40 administrative resolutions covering personnel appointments, seasonal hires, special events, bid awards, a sewer improvements contract, and a fireworks permit for the Rock Golf Club on July 5, 2025.
  • The board authorized eminent domain proceedings to acquire a leasehold interest in the easterly ground-floor unit at 127 East Main Street.
  • The board closed open session and entered executive session to discuss potential real property sale, possible Supreme Court litigation, and a BIDMA contractual matter.

Affects residents

  • A free mosaic art festival is scheduled for Sunday June 1 on Main Street from noon to 5 p.m.
  • A household waste drop-off event accepting motor oil, paint, electronics, and medications is scheduled for Saturday at the highway department yard on Osborne Avenue from 8 a.m. to 2 p.m.
