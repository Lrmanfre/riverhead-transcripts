October 15, 2026 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The board reviewed a draft environmental impact statement for golf course zoning amendments, got an update on Water District flushing work to fix discolored water in Wading River/Calverton, and advanced a series of 2027 preliminary budget amendments that reduce the proposed tax levy increase, plus numerous routine resolutions before moving to executive session.

Key actions

  • The board is expected to accept a draft environmental impact statement (DGEIS) for golf course zoning amendments, with a public hearing targeted for around December 1st.
  • The town's deputy supervisor outlined six amendments to the 2027 preliminary budget that collectively reduce the proposed tax levy increase from 2.79% to 1.79%.
  • A budget amendment sets aside $120,000 to allow the supervisor to negotiate improved dental coverage with the CSEA union.
  • Several appointments were made to the Helicopter Noise Task Force, including new and returning members.
  • One resolution (bid award, item 32) was pulled from the agenda due to an outstanding payment issue.

Discussed

  • A senior planner explained the golf course zoning proposal, which would create a new zoning district mirroring existing dimensional rules to allow golf cottage development without increasing overall residential density, tied to the town's transfer of development rights (TDR) program.
  • The Water District superintendent apologized for recent rust/discolored water complaints affecting a few hundred customers in Wading River and Calverton, attributing it to a system disturbance, and announced an engineered flushing program beginning the night of October 18th.
  • Board members discussed improving resident notification methods for water flushing events going forward.
  • Budget amendments also address merit/cost-of-living raises for highway clerical staff and reallocate street lighting reserve funds, including funding for decorative lighting near the Duck Pond following a county beautification grant approval.
