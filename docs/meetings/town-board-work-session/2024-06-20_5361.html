June 20, 2024 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Town Board work session covered proposed fee increases for the yard waste facility, monthly reports from police, code enforcement, and justice court, plus two proposed code amendments on night-time commercial paving and bed-and-breakfast zoning. The board then moved into executive session.

Key actions

  • Engineering proposed raising the annual yard waste facility permit fee from $50 to $60 and the daily fee from $15 to $20, effective January 1, 2025, citing no increases in over 20 years and rising operating costs.
  • The board discussed a draft code amendment to allow commercial parking lots with 50 or more spaces to be paved after 8 p.m. without a public hearing, requiring instead a written application submitted 10 business days in advance.
  • A cleanup amendment to the bed-and-breakfast code was presented to clarify that such facilities are only permitted in zoning districts that specifically allow them, not in every residential and commercial district.
  • The board moved into executive session to discuss a possible contractual agreement with Suffolk County.

Money

  • The yard waste permit fee increase from $50 to $60 is projected to raise total annual revenue from roughly $35,000 to $42,000.
  • Code enforcement collected $20,550 in rental permit fees during May, bringing the January-to-May total to $118,950.
  • Police reported a body-camera grant of approximately $1.47 million is expected to be paid upfront by the state rather than as a reimbursement.

Discussed

  • Board members raised concerns about political campaign signs placed on National Cemetery grounds and in public rights-of-way, and asked staff to review whether existing sign-code penalty provisions apply.
  • The police chief reported that adding officers to downtown sectors has reduced criminal incidents and overtime, and that a memorandum of understanding with a local retreat organization was signed.
  • Board members debated whether night paving rules should set a specific end-of-night hour or simply allow crews to work straight through to finish in fewer nights.

Affects residents

  • Residents who use the town yard waste facility would pay $60 per year or $20 per visit starting January 2025 if the fee increase is adopted; leaf drop-off during the fall leaf season remains free.
  • Large commercial property owners seeking to pave parking lots at night would be able to apply to the town board for approval without a formal public hearing under the proposed code change.
