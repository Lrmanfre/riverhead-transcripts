November 12, 2026 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The board reviewed a farmland preservation concept for the RGNY winery property with the Peconic Land Trust, discussed urgent plans to replace storm-damaged salt and sand barn covers with permanent steel structures, and worked through a large pre-final agenda packet before moving into executive session.

Key actions

  • The board discussed a concept plan with the Peconic Land Trust to preserve development rights on portions of the former Martha Clara/RGNY winery property along Sound Avenue, with a revised layout shifting the development area to protect roadside views.
  • The board agreed to schedule a site visit to the RGNY property, discuss parcel priorities with Suffolk County, and then order appraisals before proceeding with any purchase.
  • A resolution was pulled from the pre-final agenda to allow further board review of a request for proposals for a turnkey enforcement, permitting, and parking management solution.
  • A resolution was added authorizing the Supervisor to sign hold-harmless agreements with local fire departments for use of already-surplused town vehicles for training, replacing the need for a separate resolution each time.

Discussed

  • The Highway Superintendent and town officials reviewed the recurring failure of fabric-covered salt and sand storage structures at the Osborne Avenue highway garage, damaged repeatedly by storms including Hurricane Sandy, a 2022 nor'easter, and a recent storm, and proposed replacing them with permanent steel covers with a 30-year warranty.
  • Officials noted that FEMA funding requests related to storm damage to the salt barn structures have been submitted but reimbursement status and timing remain unclear.
  • An update on the 4-H camp project noted that a New York State open space grant application was not awarded, but a joint purchase process with Suffolk and Nassau counties is proceeding toward a planned closing in early 2027.
  • The board reviewed numerous items on the pre-final agenda, including water district maintenance contract bids, a special event permit for a race festival, an audit services agreement, a 2027 budget adoption, and a senior citizen property tax exemption extension.
