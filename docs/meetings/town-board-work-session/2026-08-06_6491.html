August 6, 2026 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Town Board held a work session covering two main topics: an agricultural worker housing application at a Sound Avenue farm, and a detailed update on Riverhead's downtown Town Square redevelopment timeline stretching from now through 2030.

Key actions

  • A senior planner presented an application to convert an existing structure at 5641 Sound Avenue into a three-bedroom agricultural worker housing unit for Sanjur Farms, with a resolution approving it expected at the next town board meeting.
  • The board discussed adding a condition requiring the farm owner to provide an employee list upon rental permit application to help verify that residents of the ag worker housing actually work on that farm.
  • The board voted to enter executive session to discuss legal matters, possible acquisition and disposition of real property, and changes in the status of employees.

Discussed

  • Town staff presented a phased construction timeline for six downtown projects: Town Square, a riverfront hotel, an adaptive playground, an amphitheater, Main Street streetscapes, and a structured parking garage, with major milestones projected through 2030.
  • A hotel foundation permit is expected within one to two weeks, and relocation of historic buildings on the East End Arts campus is planned to begin in the fall.
  • The streetscape improvement project, funded in part by a federal RAISE grant, will widen sidewalks and underground utilities from Peconic Avenue to East Avenue; staff noted the DOT permit is in hand and work could begin once the grant contract is signed.
  • A shuttle service is being considered to connect overflow parking to Main Street businesses during construction disruption, potentially funded through the parking district.
  • The structured parking garage is currently sized at roughly 504 spaces and carries approximately $17 million in available funding, with design work to advance and final construction expected between 2028 and 2030.
  • A time-lapse camera to stream downtown construction progress on the town website was proposed, and staff were asked to return monthly for public updates on the project schedule.
