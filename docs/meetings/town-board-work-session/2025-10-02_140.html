October 2, 2025 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The board reviewed a lengthy resolution list covering purchasing, personnel, local law amendments, and development agreements, and heard a planning presentation for the October 25 Halloween Fest in downtown Riverhead. The meeting closed with an executive session on potential litigation with Suffolk County Water Authority.

Key actions

  • The board reviewed approximately 48 resolutions covering topics including highway equipment purchases, personnel appointments and resignations, local law amendments to parking and bicycle/scooter regulations, a bulletproof vest grant acceptance, and a public hearing notice for 2026 CDBG funds scheduled for November 6 at 2 p.m.
  • A resolution was added to amend a prior special permit for a Jamesport restaurant, removing the previously approved 600-square-foot building expansion and catering use, and limiting total seating to 80 seats across both floors with no further expansion of parking or restaurant use permitted.
  • The board authorized a letter of intent for redevelopment of a lot adjacent to Town Hall on Griffin Avenue.
  • A fire prevention resolution was noted to set agricultural burn permit fees at zero.
  • The board moved into executive session to discuss potential litigation with Suffolk County Water Authority.

Discussed

  • A representative from the Main Street Agency presented plans for the October 25 Halloween Fest, running 4 to 10 p.m. in downtown Riverhead, featuring a parade with a potentially extended route, live performances, local food and brewery vendors in the town square, a pumpkin derby on Haley Barr Way, decorated utility poles with a prize for the best, and possible fireworks over the river at the end of the event.
  • The event organizer noted Halloween Fest is no longer run by the BID and is operating under different funding, with a dedicated website and promotion through existing Alive on 25 social media pages.
