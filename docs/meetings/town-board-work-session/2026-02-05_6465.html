February 5, 2026 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The board discussed a proposed parking restriction on Roanoke Avenue near medical offices to reduce congestion on a residential street, then moved into executive session on several legal and contractual matters.

Key actions

  • A proposed amendment to Chapter 289-12 would add a parking restriction on a portion of Roanoke Avenue on weekdays to address overflow parking from nearby medical offices; the board agreed to schedule a public hearing.
  • The board entered executive session to discuss a potential contractual agreement related to Perkins Power Station and three pending litigation matters involving a former employee, a party named Gigliotta, and Suffolk County.

Affects residents

  • Residents near Roanoke Avenue are encouraged to attend the upcoming public hearing on the proposed weekday parking restriction.
