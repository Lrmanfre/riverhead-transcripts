July 8, 2026 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board work session covered police cost recovery for private events held on public property, and a site plan application to demolish and reconstruct a rear addition at a deli on East Main Street. The board then moved into executive session.

Key actions

  • The board discussed potential code changes to require private-event organizers to cover police department costs when using town-owned public spaces, after staff reported that one event at a town park cost approximately $9,300 in police time with no reimbursement.
  • Town staff presented a site plan application for 318 East Main Street seeking approval to demolish and reconstruct an unpermitted rear commercial addition behind an existing deli, to be used for storage only.
  • The board adjourned to executive session to discuss personnel matters involving a change in employee status, the hiring of an employee, and a real property purchase matter.

Money

  • Staff reported that in 2025 the town spent approximately $112,000 on police services for events but recouped only about $25,000 to $30,000 of that amount.
  • A single private event at a town duck pond location cost approximately $9,300 in combined straight-time and overtime police pay with no cost recovery.

Discussed

  • Councilmembers discussed classifying certain large tourism-driving community events, such as the Polish festival and Duck Pond Day, as town co-sponsored events potentially exempt from police-fee requirements, while applying fees to purely private for-profit events.
  • A board member suggested a 30-day grace period before any new police-fee legislation takes effect to give event organizers already in planning a chance to adjust.
  • The planner's site plan review flagged several conditions including a deteriorated front awning, non-compliant lighting fixtures, a dilapidated dumpster enclosure, roof shingles in disrepair, an overflowing drainage structure, windows covered beyond the 10-percent code limit, and questions about two business signs for one entity.
  • Staff noted there is no crosswalk on Union Avenue connecting the deli to the municipal parking lot across the street, and suggested one be explored for pedestrian safety.

Affects residents

  • Residents may bring storm debris to the town yard waste facility and the highway yard without a yard waste sticker through Sunday July 12, provided they show a Riverhead residency ID; normal sticker rules resume Monday July 13.
  • The Live on 25 event scheduled for July 9 has been postponed while the town continues storm recovery efforts.
