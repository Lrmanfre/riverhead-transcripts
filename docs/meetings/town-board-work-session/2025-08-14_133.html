August 14, 2025 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Town Board work session covered proposed code amendments on commercial vehicle parking, e-bikes, and bicycles on Route 58, reviewed 44 resolutions for the upcoming regular meeting, and screened a recap video from National Night Out.

Key actions

  • A code amendment expanding the commercial vehicle parking prohibition on Young's Avenue to include dump trucks is set for a public hearing on August 19, with the rule expected to take effect in October.
  • A companion amendment clarifying the definition of 'local delivery' was discussed to make parking-restriction violations easier to prosecute in court.
  • Proposed code changes would prohibit all bicycles and electric scooters from riding on Route 58 sidewalks between defined endpoints due to safety concerns in that high-traffic corridor.
  • A separate amendment would classify e-bikes into three state DMV categories and restrict Class 2 and Class 3 electric-assist bicycles from roads with speed limits over 30 mph, from bike lanes, and from the bike path; penalties would range from up to $250 for a first offense to up to $500 for a third or more.
  • The board reviewed 44 resolutions for the August 19 regular meeting, including accepting a donated hockey facility valued at roughly $2.9 million from the Peconic Hockey Foundation, applying $525,000 in proceeds from the sale of 201 Howell Avenue to repay a general-fund loan used for Town Square demolition costs, and authorizing road-resurfacing grants under CHIPS, PAVE NY, and POP NY programs.
  • The board noted that a volunteer EMT course held at Town Hall last year produced approximately 25 new certified EMTs, and a license agreement for a second year of that program is on the resolution list.
  • The session closed with the board moving into executive session to discuss a leave of absence, a class-action grievance, pending litigation, and several contractual agreements.
