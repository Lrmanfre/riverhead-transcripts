October 23, 2025 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Town Board work session covered planned renovations to Bayberry Park's parking lot, adoption of NFPA codes as a technical reference standard, and a clarification about the downtown redevelopment pre-possession agreement. The board then moved to executive session on a contractual matter.

Key actions

  • The board discussed a plan to fully mill, regrade, repave, and restripe the parking lot at Bayberry Park (also called Police Officers Memorial Park) in Wading River, with a budget of approximately $325,000.
  • Funding for the parking lot project was identified as coming primarily from cannabis tax revenue and secondarily from park and recreation fees, with a board member noting neither source is direct taxpayer money.
  • Engineers are exploring adding a paved service road and small accessible parking area at the lower level of the park to address ADA accessibility concerns within the existing budget.
  • Work is expected to begin within roughly two weeks, with a goal of completing the project before paving plants close around the end of the year.
  • The board discussed formally adopting NFPA codes (with exceptions for NFPA 1 and NFPA 5000) as a supplemental technical reference standard to strengthen enforcement of building and safety requirements, including for battery energy storage systems, emergency radio coverage, and cannabis facilities.
  • The town attorney clarified that a pre-possession and lease agreement related to the downtown redevelopment project was not a new or unauthorized action, stating it was explicitly contemplated in the previously public master developer agreement approved after a qualified and eligible hearing.
  • The board entered executive session to discuss a contractual matter involving a named individual and town counsel.
