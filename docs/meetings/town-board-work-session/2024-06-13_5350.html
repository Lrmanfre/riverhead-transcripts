June 13, 2024 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting

Summary AI

The Town Board work session featured a PSEG Long Island presentation on new time-of-day electricity rates and reviewed a draft food truck ordinance, then read through 33 resolutions for the upcoming meeting.

Key actions

  • The board is set to vote at the June 18 meeting on an MOA that includes recognizing Juneteenth (June 19) as a paid holiday, with Town Hall expected to close that day.
  • A $500,000 state grant contract for the Riverfront Adaptive Children's Playground was placed on the resolution list for approval.
  • The board directed staff to publish and post a public hearing notice for a proposed code amendment to regulate food trucks, restricting them to properties with an existing restaurant, brewery, winery, or similar use, and prohibiting operation within 500 feet of a competing food establishment.
  • Bids for residential solid waste collection and disposal were rejected and the town will re-bid with revised specifications, with new bid documents posted by June 27.
  • A public hearing was scheduled for July 16 on the Hildreth Real Estate Advisors site plan at 1001 Scott Avenue, Calverton, after the board assumed lead agency and issued a negative declaration under SEQRA.
  • Preliminary approval was placed on the resolution list for a commercial warehouse site plan at 901 Berman Boulevard, Calverton.
  • The board will hold a public hearing on July 2 on proposed amendments to the Community Preservation Fund Water Quality Improvement and Pollution Prevention Project Plan.
  • The board entered executive session to discuss a possible MOA or STIP, a personnel disciplinary matter, and possible litigation.

Discussed

  • PSEG Long Island representatives explained the new residential time-of-day rate program, which sets peak pricing from 3–7 p.m. weekdays and offers up to a 40% overnight discount; customers who do not save in the first 12 months receive a bill credit, and migration of roughly 31,000 customers began this month.
  • The PSEG app's rate-comparison tool lets customers see which plan would have saved them the most based on 12 months of smart-meter data and allows enrollment directly in the app.
