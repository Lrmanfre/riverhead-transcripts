October 9, 2025 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The board heard a brief planning update on a new horse barn at Timothy Hill Children's Ranch and made community announcements before moving into executive session on three litigation matters.

Key actions

  • A senior planner presented a draft resolution finding that the 1977 special permit for Timothy Hill Children's Ranch does not require amendment for the ranch to replace a demolished horse barn with a new approximately 6,500-square-foot barn, which would allow Planning Board administrative approval.
  • The board entered executive session to discuss litigation involving the New York State Office of Cannabis Management, the Town of Southampton, and a party identified as American Breeders.

Affects residents

  • Town offices will be closed Monday, October 13 (Columbus Day) and reopen Tuesday, October 14 at 8 a.m.
  • The Recreation Department is hosting a repair cafe and Halloween costume exchange at the George Young Community Center and Town Hall respectively.
