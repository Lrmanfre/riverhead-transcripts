June 26, 2025 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting
CivicClerk page

Summary AI

The town board held a work session covering a detailed presentation on the planned Main Street streetscape redesign funded by a federal RAISE grant, reviewed a mixed-use building addition application near McDermott Avenue with unresolved parking questions, and heard a battery energy storage system site plan application for a small facility at EpCal.

Key actions

  • The board received an update on the East Main Street streetscape improvement project covering approximately 770 linear feet from Roanoke Avenue to East Avenue, funded in part by a $750,000 DRI grant for design work.
  • Staff reported that a federal RAISE grant contract of approximately $10 million is expected to be executed within roughly 60 days, which would allow the streetscape and related downtown projects to move toward construction.
  • The streetscape plan includes widened sidewalks, shortened crosswalks, narrowed travel lanes, new street lighting, street trees, buried utility lines from East Avenue to a driveway near the diner, and a HAWK pedestrian signal near the Suffolk Theater.
  • Approximately 11 on-street parking spaces on Main Street would be removed under the current plan.
  • The board reviewed a site plan application for a four-story mixed-use addition at 301 East Main Street and 12 McDermott Avenue that would add six two-bedroom apartments and roughly 1,000 square feet of ground-floor commercial space to a previously approved building.
  • Staff noted the combined project would generate a code-based need for approximately 13 additional parking spaces while providing only two, and raised the possibility of a payment-in-lieu-of-parking requirement; no decision was made.
  • The board reviewed a site plan and special permit application for a five-megawatt battery energy storage system at 221 Scott Avenue in Calverton; no action was taken pending comments from Manorville Fire Department and the state DEC.
  • The board was presented with a resolution list that included accepting a $1.4 million New York State ESD grant for the Town Square Riverfront Amphitheater and approving fireworks applications for events on July 18 and July 19.

Money

  • A $1.4 million New York State ESD grant for the Town Square Riverfront Amphitheater was listed for acceptance by resolution.
  • A payment-in-lieu-of-parking figure of roughly $12,000–$15,000 per space was discussed as a possible condition for residential developments that provide no on-site parking, though no rate has been formally adopted.

Discussed

  • Board members raised questions about whether plowable speed-table crosswalks like those used in Westhampton Beach could be permitted by the state DOT on Route 25; staff agreed to seek an answer.
  • The town will take over maintenance of the improved Main Street segment from Roanoke Avenue to East Avenue as a condition of the state permit, while the DOT retains jurisdiction over signage standards.
  • A planned 504-space modular parking garage north of Main Street was described as expandable and expected to be designed once the RAISE grant contract is executed; it will be a paid facility.
  • A shuttle service to bring visitors from outlying parking areas to Main Street during construction was mentioned as under consideration in coordination with the chamber of commerce.
