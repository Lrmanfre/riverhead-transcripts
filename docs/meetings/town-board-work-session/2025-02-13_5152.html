Summary AI

The Town Board work session covered a proposed paid parking pilot program for downtown Riverhead and reviewed a large slate of resolutions scheduled for the next board meeting, including zoning amendments tied to the 2024 Comprehensive Plan update.

Key actions

  • The board reached general consensus to move forward with signing an agreement with a parking technology vendor to launch a paid parking test in downtown Riverhead, likely in late spring, with details on lot selection, signage, and timing to be finalized after a follow-up meeting with the parking district.
  • A public hearing on amending zoning code regulations for Tier 2 battery energy storage systems was scheduled for March 4 at 2 p.m.
  • A public hearing on amending zoning code regulations for wireless communications towers and antennas was scheduled for March 4 at 2 p.m.
  • A public hearing on amending zoning code sign regulations was scheduled for March 4 at 2 p.m.
  • A public hearing on amending Pine Barrens Overlay District clearing and open space standards was scheduled for March 4 at 2 p.m.
  • Public hearings on site plan review extensions and a signs code amendment were also scheduled for an upcoming board meeting.
  • The board noted a resolution authorizing free beach stickers for veterans and volunteer fire and ambulance department members.
  • The board went into executive session to discuss personnel and real property matters.

Discussed

  • Board members debated whether a single-lot parking test would yield accurate data, with some concern that drivers would simply avoid a paid lot and park elsewhere for free; a parking consultant is expected to advise on scope at the upcoming parking district meeting.
  • A resolution amending the board's own rules and procedures was pulled from the agenda for further review before a future meeting.
