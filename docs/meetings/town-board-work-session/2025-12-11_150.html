December 11, 2025 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board work session covered a proposed golf cottage code amendment, a groundbreaking announcement for the new Town Square, and previewed a lengthy consent agenda of routine resolutions ahead of a future regular meeting.

Key actions

  • A groundbreaking ceremony for the demolition of 127 East Main Street to make way for the new Riverhead Town Square is scheduled for Friday, December 12 at noon.
  • The board reviewed a revised definition of 'golf cottage' under Chapter 301-3, with the maximum unit size increased from 600 to 1,200 square feet, no limit on length of stay, and a prohibition on using cottages to establish permanent residency.
  • Golf cottages would require one transfer of development right per unit, up to a maximum of one per hole; currently only Friars Head Golf Course and Beany Hollow Club are in eligible zoning districts.
  • The board directed staff to prepare a resolution scheduling a public hearing on the golf cottage amendment, likely in January.
  • The board reviewed proposed code changes to the Business Center and Shopping Center Zoning Use Districts, including expanding permitted uses in shopping centers and bringing the 35-foot unoccupied front yard requirement into supplementary guidelines.
  • The board discussed land-banking and parking reduction provisions already in the town code as tools for redeveloping underutilized commercial parking areas along Route 58.
  • A large block of consent resolutions was previewed, including a new CSEA contract for 2026–2029, multiple Water District bid extensions, a PFAS treatment bid for a grant-funded project, and agreements related to the proposed East End Arts Pavilion.
  • The board moved into executive session to discuss personnel, employment, and several contractual matters.
