July 11, 2024 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board work session covered proposed amendments to a drag racing license agreement at EPCAL to add drifting and go-kart events, a code change to parking rules, a special permit application to convert an office back to a single-family home, and a new town social media initiative called 'Riverhead in Action.'

Key actions

  • The board agreed to pursue an addendum to the existing EPCAL/racetrack license agreement to allow drifting and go-kart events to run concurrently with already-approved drag racing dates, pending submission of a revised site plan and town board approval before the first event on August 17.
  • The board indicated support for removing the 24-hour exception from the front-yard parking code (Chapter 217-67) to make violations easier to prosecute, while retaining prosecutorial discretion to reduce or dismiss fines.
  • A special permit application to convert 431 Griffin Avenue from office use back to a single-family dwelling was advanced, with a public hearing scheduled for August 6.
  • The board introduced 'Riverhead in Action,' a monthly video series to be posted on a new town YouTube channel, Facebook, and Instagram highlighting town departments, events, and local businesses.
  • The board noted a resolution authorizing highway asphalt paving contracts would be added for ratification to allow road paving work to begin.
  • The open session closed and the board moved into executive session to discuss a personnel matter, a seawall contract, and two legal matters involving real property and litigation with the DEC.
