July 18, 2024 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting

Summary AI

The July 18 work session covered monthly reports from the Police Department, Code Enforcement, Justice Court, and Town Clerk, then introduced a new citizen-staff committee formed to plan future development of the EPCAL property, and concluded with updates from the downtown BID and East End Arts.

Key actions

  • The board entered executive session at the close of the meeting to discuss a personnel matter involving a change in employee status.
  • A new volunteer advisory body called the Citizens' Cooperative Development Team (CCDT) was publicly introduced; it is chaired by the Town Supervisor and is tasked with planning and community engagement for the EPCAL property ahead of resolution of pending litigation.
  • A public Gmail address—revisionepcal@gmail.com—was announced for residents to submit comments, questions, or suggestions about EPCAL redevelopment.
  • The Police Department reported receiving $1.47 million in state funding for body cameras, which is now held in a town account pending vendor meetings and a PBA contract on usage.

Money

  • Code Enforcement collected $32,000 in fees and $13,600 in rental fees for June, bringing the year-to-date rental fee total to approximately $151,550.
  • The Town Clerk's office collected approximately $10,523 in fees last month, of which roughly $9,199 was the town's local share.

Discussed

  • The Police Department noted a transition from the UCR to the NIBRS crime-reporting system, which may affect how criminal incident totals are counted; June was the department's first NIBRS submission to the state with an error rate under 2%.
  • A board member asked Justice Court staff to begin tracking and reporting how mandatory surcharge revenues are distributed among designated funds, such as water quality and recreation.
  • East End Arts introduced its new executive director and announced a summer fundraising event on August 3rd; the organization also noted ongoing plans tied to the Town Square redevelopment.
  • The downtown BID reported that the July 5th Alive on 25 event was successful and announced a laser light show planned for the August 9th edition.
