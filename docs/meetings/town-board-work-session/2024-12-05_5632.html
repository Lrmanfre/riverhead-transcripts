December 5, 2024 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The board held a brief work session focused on a proposed water rate adjustment, then entered executive session to discuss a contractual matter with the Jazz Loft.

Discussed

  • The water district superintendent presented a proposal for a small incremental rate increase affecting only the highest-volume users, raising the per-thousand-gallon charge above 90,000 gallons from $2.75 to $3.00, which would add roughly $2.50 to the largest users' bills.
  • The presentation also included a proposed $50 fee for on-site meter data downloads and a new hourly labor charge for water district staff called to assist large commercial users with troubleshooting.
  • The board entered executive session to discuss contractual matters between the Town of Riverhead and the Jazz Loft.
