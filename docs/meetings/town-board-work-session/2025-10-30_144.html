A brief work session covered 20 resolutions ahead of the November 6 meeting, then the board moved into executive session on a contractual matter. No substantive debate occurred on most items.

Key actions

  • The board advanced a resolution to hire an administrative law judge to hear contested stop-arm bus camera cases, replacing the Justice Court process that handled those hearings.
  • A resolution was advanced to publish notice of a proposed stop sign at the intersection of Old Stone Road and Timber Drive, following a three-month traffic study.
  • The board entered executive session to discuss a contractual matter involving the Jazz Loft.

Affects residents

  • Trick-or-treating is expected to end at 8 p.m. on Halloween; early voting continues through Sunday, November 2, at Town Hall.
