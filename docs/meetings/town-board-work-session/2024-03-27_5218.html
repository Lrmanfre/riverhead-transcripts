March 27, 2024 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Town Board held a brief work session focused on committee updates and a preview of 48 draft resolutions headed to a vote, covering personnel appointments, 2023 capital project closures, agritourism zoning amendments, and several new agreements and contracts.

Key actions

  • The board previewed a resolution authorizing a SEQRA determination for pre-construction activities related to a proposed First Street parking garage, described as a necessary step in the grant process.
  • Two resolutions were listed to schedule public hearings on proposed amendments to Chapter 301 zoning code related to agritourism supplemental regulations.
  • A resolution was listed to authorize an agreement with an engineering firm for the downtown Town Square project, and a separate surveying services authorization for the same project was also included.
  • A resolution would authorize the chief of police to execute an agreement with the Riverhead Community Awareness Program (CAP).
  • Resolution 9 would reappoint three part-time police officers who completed the part-time police academy on their own time without pay.
  • Resolutions 10 through 30 consist entirely of 2023 capital project closures across sewer, water, and general capital funds.
  • One personnel resolution (originally listed as number 5, a salary increase for a senior citizen program supervisor) was pulled and will be replaced with an appointment of a call-in senior recreation leader.

Discussed

  • Committee updates included: a barricade on the EPCAL bike path will be removed, Reeves Beach has reopened with a refurbished gazebo, a farmland preservation appraisal was authorized for a Main Road property in Aquebogue, the Perkins Generating Plant demolition has been postponed and an on-site visit is planned with interested parties considering a learning center use, and eight Community Preservation Fund open space signs are being installed around town.
  • A council member reported that a vape scan conducted with police found THC products at one shop, and police were directed to follow up.
  • A council member described ongoing discussions about Community Distributed Generation and Community Choice Aggregation solar programs that could benefit homeowners, renters, and small businesses without requiring panel installation.
  • The Business Advisory Committee is working with the Building Department to review situations where permit applicants face unexpected additional engineering fees that delay their projects.
  • The Receiver of Taxes office is working with IT and a bank to enable online bill payment, including an in-office computer with step-by-step instructions for residents who need assistance.
