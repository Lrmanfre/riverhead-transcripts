May 8, 2025 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting

Summary AI

The Town Board work session covered a National Grid compressed natural gas facility special permit at 8 Mill Road, reviewed public comments on the Island Water Park conditional negative declaration, and heard committee updates from board members before moving to executive session.

Key actions

  • The board reviewed a special permit and site plan application for a National Grid compressed natural gas truck staging facility at 8 Mill Road, with a negative declaration and a public hearing scheduled for May 21st.
  • Staff recommended removing two conditions from the Island Water Park conditional negative declaration — requirements for Suffolk County Department of Health and DEC approvals — because those are approvals the applicant is already legally obligated to obtain.
  • The board discussed potentially revising Town Code section 225-11B-5 to clarify that the 50-or-more parking space threshold applies to new construction, not existing facilities undergoing improvements.
  • The board went into executive session to discuss potential litigation, acquisition of real property, a personnel matter involving a change in employee status, and professional services contracts.

Discussed

  • The National Grid facility would operate only December through February to supplement natural gas supply during peak cold-weather demand, with up to 24 compressed natural gas trucks staged on site.
  • A 30-foot access easement on the south side of the National Grid property raises future traffic safety concerns at the Mill Road and West Main Street intersection; staff asked the applicant to explore relocating it to align with the site driveway, which the applicant said may be addressed in a future application.
  • Staff noted three public comment letters on the Island Water Park CND raised concerns about go-kart noise impacts on grassland birds, but coordination with DEC during the review period produced no comments on that issue and separation distance from identified habitats is approximately 1,150 feet.

Affects residents

  • Beach parking stickers are available at Town Hall; the final drive-through pickup is this Saturday from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m., and a sticker is required to park at town beaches year-round.
  • Tax Grievance Day is Tuesday, May 20th at Town Hall, with the Review Board meeting from 10 a.m. to noon and 6 p.m. to 8 p.m.
  • Free pre-cancer screenings and health outreach events have been offered to the Hispanic and Latino community through the town's diversity committee in partnership with Northwell Health.
  • A free lead pipe replacement program is available to residents with old lead service lines coming into their homes.
  • Upcoming public events include the senior prom on May 9th, a CPR saves ceremony on May 19th at 6:30 p.m. at Town Hall, free public ice skating sessions in May and June at the town rink, and the CAP committee walk on June 6th.
