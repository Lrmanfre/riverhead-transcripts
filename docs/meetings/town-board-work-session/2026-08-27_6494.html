August 27, 2026 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page

Summary AI

The board reviewed a maternity ward expansion and facade renovation plan for Peconic Bay Medical Center and held a lengthy, contentious discussion with Riverhead School District representatives over funding for School Resource Officers (SROs), reaching no resolution but agreeing to schedule a follow-up meeting.

Key actions

  • The board reviewed and appeared receptive to a draft administrative approval for Peconic Bay Medical Center's (Northwell Health) maternity ward relocation and facade renovation project, which will be placed on the packet for the next meeting.
  • The town board and Riverhead Central School District officials discussed the expired agreement on cost-sharing for two School Resource Officers but did not reach a resolution; both sides agreed to schedule a further meeting involving the full school board before the school year starts.
  • A slate of routine resolutions was presented for the upcoming meeting, including retirements of a police detective, a police officer, and a senior justice court clerk, a bond resolution for an ambulance building project, and authorization to bid on water main installations for the Riverhead Water District.
  • The board voted to enter executive session to discuss the potential hiring of an employee with the chief engineer.

Discussed

  • Northwell Health representatives described plans to reskin the hospital building's facade, add new windows, screen rooftop and ground-level mechanical equipment, and create a new entrance and parking layout for the relocated maternity/Women's Health Center, while keeping the same distance from the road.
  • School district officials proposed reducing the town's SRO cost-sharing to 50% of base salaries only (or alternatively, 50% of full costs for just one officer), citing budget constraints and disputing that they had ever committed to fully funding the program.
  • Town board members argued that SRO costs should be a school district budget responsibility, noting that not all town residents attend the Riverhead School District, and suggested the district seek additional funding from Southampton due to shared students from Riverside and Flanders.
  • Both sides disagreed over the history of the prior cost-sharing arrangement and communication breakdowns regarding scheduling a meeting before the school year began.

Affects residents

  • The status of School Resource Officers in Riverhead schools for the upcoming school year remains unresolved pending further negotiation between the town and school district.
  • Renovations at Peconic Bay Medical Center are expected to begin soon, including a new maternity/Women's Health entrance and updated building facade.
