July 24, 2025 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Town Board held a work session covering a CSEA union grievance hearing on flex-time agreements and a review of proposed increases to three sets of town code violation fines, before moving into executive session.

Key actions

  • The board heard presentations from both the CSEA and town counsel on a grievance appeal concerning termination of flex-time agreements, and will issue a decision within 20 days.
  • Town counsel argued the grievance is moot because the original termination letters were rescinded and reissued with proper 15-day notice, and urged the board to deny the grievance.
  • The board discussed raising fines for Chapter 301 site plan and illegal rental violations from a $0–$1,000 range (unchanged since 1989) to a $1,000–$5,000 range.
  • The board discussed raising overweight vehicle fines under Chapter 289-29 (unchanged since 2001) to a range of $500–$1,000 for a first offense, $1,000–$2,000 for a second, and $2,000–$4,000 for a third.
  • The board discussed raising fire marshal violation fines under Chapter 231-66 from the current $250–$2,500 range to $1,000–$5,000.
  • Staff noted plans to systematically review all town code fines annually to keep pace with inflation and cost of enforcement.
  • The board closed open session and entered executive session to discuss a possible sale of real property.

Affects residents

  • A cooling center at the Riverhead Senior Center will be open Friday, July 25, from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. due to expected heat near 100 degrees.
  • The annual Paddle Battle on the Peconic River is scheduled for Saturday, July 26, beginning at 9:15 a.m. near Heidi Bear Way.
