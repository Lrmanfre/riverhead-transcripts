February 12, 2026 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Town Board work session covered fire safety code amendments, a frozen pipe closure at the George Young Community Center, and reviewed 36 draft resolutions ahead of the next board meeting.

Key actions

  • Two proposed code amendments were discussed: one updating fire protection system maintenance and inspection documentation requirements under Chapter 231, and one creating a new change-of-tenancy inspection requirement triggered when a commercial space has been vacant 30 days or more.
  • The board indicated the fire safety amendments are ready to be published for public notice.
  • A resolution to adopt a local law creating a property tax exemption for surviving spouses of police officers killed in the line of duty was queued for potential adoption following a scheduled public hearing.
  • A resolution to reject bids and rebid PFAS treatment at a town water district well was reviewed, with the purchasing director citing prevailing wage law thresholds as the reason for rebidding.
  • The board agreed to move discussion of a playground agreement with a landscape architecture firm into executive session under contractual matters.
  • The board voted to enter executive session to address personnel disciplinary matters, litigation updates, and a contractual matter involving a landscape architecture firm.

Money

  • A third-party fire protection compliance tracking platform was described as self-funding through fees, with the town collecting a portion and the vendor handling outreach and documentation at no additional staffing cost to the town.
  • A resolution authorizes rejection and rebidding of a PFAS water treatment project due to prevailing wage law requirements triggered by the total project cost.

Affects residents

  • The George Young Community Center is closed this week due to a frozen pipe break; affected programs including youth cheerleading, yoga, a slime time event, Kids Night Out, and February break camp have been canceled or relocated to Town Hall or Pulaski Street Elementary School.
  • Town Hall will be closed Monday, February 16, in observance of Presidents Day.
  • The Disability Advisory Committee announced its next public meeting is May 5 at 5 p.m., open to all community members.
