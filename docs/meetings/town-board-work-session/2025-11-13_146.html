November 13, 2025 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Town Board held a brief work session, reading a short list of resolutions before moving into executive session to discuss tax litigation and a personnel matter.

Key actions

  • The board reviewed resolutions covering trust fund expenditures, fixed asset removal, staff appointments and promotions, appraisals of tanks and land, a one-year extension of a federal water-monitoring equipment license at Wading River boat ramp, designation of the Jazz Loft Inc. as a qualified sponsor for redevelopment of the Vale-Levitt Music Hall at 18 Peconic Avenue, and a Lions Club Christmas parade event on December 6 centered around Town Hall.
  • The board entered executive session to discuss tax litigation involving Traditional Links and a separate personnel matter.

Discussed

  • A fire at Wells Farm in the Aquebogue-Jamesport area caused minor smoke-inhalation injuries and loss of some farm animals; first responders were credited with saving others.
  • The Lions Club Christmas parade route will run down Main Street and end at Town Hall, where vendors and a tree lighting are planned for December 6.
