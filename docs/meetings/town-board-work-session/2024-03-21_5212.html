March 21, 2024 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting

Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board work session covered monthly police and court reports, a private RV park proposal at EPCAL, significant revisions to the agritourism overlay code, and several upcoming community events including a job fair, river cleanup, and Shakespeare in the Park.

Key actions

  • The board directed the justice court attorney to begin recording instances where judges do not impose minimum mandatory fines, for future tracking.
  • A new full-time code enforcement officer is set to begin work on April 1, with two additional positions — one code officer and one clerical — still open.
  • The board confirmed that at least one new code enforcement vehicle is needed and that funding has already been allocated in the budget.
  • Public bathrooms on Heidi Bear Way and near Grandville Park are expected to reopen seasonally within approximately one month.
  • The board reiterated that short-term rentals, including Airbnb listings, require a minimum 29-day rental period under town code.

Money

  • Justice Court collected approximately $5,600 in February 2024, including $3,400 in court collections, a $2,000 default judgment, and $200 in preservation fines.
  • The town noted it is reevaluating and increasing fees related to sand removal.

Discussed

  • A private developer and attorney presented a concept for a private RV park at EPCAL in Calverton, proposing a lease with the town retaining ownership, up to 100–125 RV slots, amenities including a clubhouse and glamping units, and a reserved percentage of slots for Riverhead residents; board members raised concerns about sand mining for a proposed lake, DEC bird and grassland mitigation requirements, clearing limits, and proximity to runways.
  • Proposed revisions to the agritourism overlay code would set yield at 1,500 square feet of floor area per acre of project area with a 100-acre minimum, cap hotel and resort rooms at 150, raise farm building height limits from 25 to 35 feet, require a 500-foot front yard setback along Sound Avenue, and impose a $1,000-per-day penalty for agricultural non-production exceeding nine months; farming community representatives expressed general support but requested one additional Agricultural Advisory Committee review before publication.
  • The OpenGov platform implementation remains delayed due to data transfer issues and is still in progress.

Affects residents

  • A town job fair is planned for May 23rd, 1–4 p.m. at Town Square, with the Suffolk Theatre as a rain location and a New York Blood Center blood drive bus on site.
  • A community river cleanup along the Peconic River is proposed for Saturday, May 18th, in partnership with the Rotary Club, Long Island Aquarium, and local paddleboard and kayak groups.
  • Northeast Stage's non-profit production of Othello is proposed for Wednesday, July 24th at Grand Jewel Park, with ASL interpretation and Spanish language programming planned.
  • An Anti-Litter Committee townwide cleanup is scheduled for Sunday, April 28th, with materials available for pickup from 9 to 11 a.m. and approximately 20–25 volunteers signed up.
