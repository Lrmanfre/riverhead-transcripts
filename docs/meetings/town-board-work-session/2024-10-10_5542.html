October 10, 2024 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The board discussed a 30-day paid parking pilot in one downtown lot and a new Limited English Proficiency policy, then reviewed a slate of resolutions scheduled for the October 16 regular meeting.

Key actions

  • The board agreed to move forward with a 30-day paid parking pilot in the lot behind Diggers on West Main Street, to begin roughly ten days after a resolution is adopted on October 16.
  • The pilot will charge approximately one dollar per hour; businesses may optionally validate parking for customers, meaning the business absorbs the cost.
  • A dedicated website and a 24/7 text-help number will be set up before the pilot launches to inform businesses and the public.
  • The board reviewed a draft Limited English Proficiency policy that would extend translation services town-wide, including expanding the police department's existing language line to all town offices.
  • A resolution was flagged to rescind a 2023 ARPA-funded budget adoption for software that was never drawn down, as part of obligating all American Rescue Plan funds before the December 31 deadline.
  • A resolution authorizes the supervisor to execute an agreement with a fire rescue equipment vendor to get in queue for a new ambulance chassis and box retrofit, with delivery estimated one and a half to two and a half years out.
  • Public hearings on dock regulations and a residential parking code amendment were scheduled for November 7 at 2 p.m.

Discussed

  • Staff noted the downtown parking garage project has over seventeen million dollars in grant awards toward an estimated twenty-three million dollar cost, but cannot spend any funds until a federal contract is signed, with shovels in the ground estimated roughly two years away.
  • A Congressional Medal of Honor belonging to Private First Class Garfield Langhorne will be on display in Town Hall from October 11 through Veterans Day, on loan from his family.
