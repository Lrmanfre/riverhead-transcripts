September 10, 2026 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page

Summary AI

The Town Board reviewed a draft downtown parking utilization plan presented by a parking consultant, covering paid parking pilot concepts, a possible downtown shuttle, and reserved parking ideas, then acted on a routine slate of resolutions including an extension of the school resource officer program funded by town taxes.

Discussed

  • A parking consultant presented draft, non-final concepts for a townwide parking utilization plan, including designating lots 1, 2, and 4 downtown for paid parking (roughly 608 of about 2,881 total spaces) seasonally from May through October at an estimated $1.25–1.50 per hour.
  • Ideas discussed included reserved/priority parking near the Suffolk Theater using license-plate recognition technology instead of gates, restaurant parking validation programs, and a possible free downtown micro-shuttle funded by parking revenue.
  • Board members asked that future sessions address Polish Town-area parking and businesses, impacts on Summerwind apartment residents, employee parking permits, and concerns raised by the parking district advisory committee, including a request that a hotel operator attend an upcoming parking committee meeting.
  • The board discussed progress on a planned parking garage, noting a pending federal grant contract expected soon and $2 million in county funding available for design once that contract is signed; lot 4 was also identified as a flood-prone area that could incorporate flood mitigation if redeveloped.
  • The town attorney reported that the Riverhead school district agreed to pay $200,000 toward the school resource officer program for the 2026-27 school year, with the town covering an estimated $150,000 balance; some board members objected that this shifts costs onto town taxpayers, including residents in other school districts, without STAR tax exemption benefit, and called for a board-to-board meeting with school officials before the next budget cycle.

Key actions

  • The board reviewed a long list of routine resolutions for an upcoming meeting, including capital project budget adjustments (sewer district, Wading River Duck Ponds restoration, HVAC replacement at George Young Community Center), several police and staff retirements/appointments, and multiple water district bid notices.
  • The board set a public hearing for October 6 on the 2027 Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) funds and requested that CDBG applications be distributed to all board members in advance, with individual votes rather than a single bundled resolution.
  • A resolution amending fire prevention fees and one related to CDA matters were pulled from consideration at the town attorney's and CDA office's request.
  • The board authorized a public notice to consider a local law amending the town's vehicle, traffic, and parking regulations (Chapter 289, Article 2).
