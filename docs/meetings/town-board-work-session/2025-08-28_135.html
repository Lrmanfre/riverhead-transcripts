The board reviewed a special permit amendment for a proposed fine dining restaurant at the Jamesport Manor Inn and walked through a slate of administrative resolutions ahead of a formal vote.

Key actions

  • The board discussed a special permit amendment application for 370 Manor Lane, Jamesport, seeking to allow use of the second floor of the Jamesport Manor Inn for restaurant seating; staff recommended scheduling a public hearing at the second September meeting.
  • The proposed restaurant, to be called 18 Bay, would operate with no more than approximately 82 seats, no catering or wedding events, and no outdoor amplified music.
  • A resolution to adopt a budget for capital improvements at Two Bears Park was reviewed.
  • A resolution to use ambulance district billing proceeds for architectural services for a new RVAC building was reviewed; a public fundraising event is planned for October 18.
  • Several personnel resolutions were reviewed, including provisional appointment of an account clerk, a salary increase for a maintenance mechanic, promotion of a building inspector to senior building inspector, two resignations, and multiple appointments and reappointments to the Agricultural Advisory Committee.
  • Several local law amendments were reviewed, covering fire prevention penalties, vehicle and traffic regulations, noise and property maintenance, and the Community Benefit Zoning use district.
  • A resolution authorizing demolition or abatement of an unsafe structure at 39 Pie Lane, Jamesport, was reviewed.
  • The board moved into executive session to discuss litigation related to a building matter.
