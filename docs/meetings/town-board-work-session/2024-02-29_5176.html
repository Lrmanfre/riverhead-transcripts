February 29, 2024 — Town Board Work Session

Town Board Work Session Meeting

Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board held a Leap Day work session covering committee updates, a warehouse project at EPCAL, labor-management relations, and a slate of resolutions including an ambulance facility planning transfer and cannabis zoning changes.

Key actions

  • The board accepted a fund transfer within the ambulance district budget to begin drawing up plans for a new ambulance facility at the current site.
  • The board assumed lead agency status and issued a negative declaration under SEQRA for an 84,000-square-foot warehouse proposed at 901–923 Berman Boulevard in the EPCAL planned industrial park.
  • A public hearing on the Berman Boulevard warehouse site plan was scheduled for March 19 at 6:05 p.m.
  • The board approved re-bidding the Meeting House Creek stormwater wetland and habitat restoration project after the first round came in over budget and lacked required MWBE provisions.
  • A local law amending the town zoning code related to cannabis was adopted.
  • Preliminary site plan approval with conditions was granted for a two-story restaurant proposed at 38 McDiarmid Avenue overlooking the river and marina.
  • The board accepted the retirement of the Senior Citizens Center manager, Debbie Schwartz.
  • A grant application to the U.S. Department of Energy's EECBG program for approximately $76,000 for riverfront lighting improvements was authorized.

Affects residents

  • All town tax exemption paperwork must be submitted to the Assessor's Office at Town Hall by 4:30 p.m. on March 1 — this covers all exemptions, not only STAR.
  • The anti-litter committee's spring cleanup is scheduled for Sunday, April 28 at 9 a.m., meeting at Town Hall.
  • The Riverhead Rotary Garden Festival and an East End Latin Festival were approved as special events, both scheduled around May.
  • Tax grievance day before the Board of Assessment Review is Tuesday, March 21.
  • Online rental permit applications through OpenGov are expected to go live around April 1, allowing credit card or bank account payments.
  • East Creek has been reopened for clamming by Riverhead residents, and the town's clam seeding program was described as successful.
