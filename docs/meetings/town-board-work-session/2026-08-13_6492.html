August 13, 2026 — Town Board Work Session

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board held a short work session on August 13, 2026, voting on 45 resolutions covering budget transfers, personnel, grants, sewer fees, downtown revitalization, and community events before entering executive session.

Key actions

  • A new $3,500 penalty was added for property owners who connect to the Riverhead Sewer District without required authorization; three such unauthorized connections reportedly occurred in the prior two weeks.
  • The board moved to establish a downpayment assistance program for eligible first-time homebuyers in partnership with Suffolk County, offering up to $28,000 in additional town funds on a first-come, first-served basis.
  • Resolutions were approved to publish public hearing notices on fireworks permit oversight moving to the fire marshal's office, a sewer district extension to 840 Old Country Road, and a Monroe balancing test hearing for a Suffolk County Water Authority North Fork water main project scheduled for September 1st.

Discussed

  • Two upcoming license agreements with Riverhead Volunteer Ambulance Corps—for use of the armory during facility construction and for office space in the town hall annex basement—were noted as expected to be ready for the next town board meeting.
