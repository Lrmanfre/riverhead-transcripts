The Planning Board granted preliminary approval for the 16-lot Summerwind Farms residential subdivision and discussed a proposed zoning code change to streamline site plan review from three steps to two, plus early-stage sketch plan feedback for a proposed industrial subdivision of the former Suffolk Cement sand mine site.

Key actions

  • The board approved an extension of the minor subdivision approval for a applicant named Vincent Franklin (Resolution 2026-017).
  • The board approved preliminary plat for the Summerwind Farms major subdivision, a 16-lot clustered residential development on an approximately 30-acre parcel on Peconic Bay Boulevard, with two agricultural reserve lots (Resolution 2026-018).
  • Preliminary approval conditions include a prohibition on commercial cannabis cultivation on the agricultural reserve lots, a restriction that lots may not be further subdivided, and language that no structures or impediments may be placed within the 16-foot wide right-of-way on the eastern property boundary.
  • A parks and recreation fee of $5,000 per new residential lot was assessed on 15 of the 16 lots (totaling $75,000), with the existing house on lot 16 exempted.
  • Water infrastructure for Summerwind Farms is estimated at $722,000, with a separate key money fee of $5,000 per lot due to the water district.
  • A four-foot split rail fence along the right-of-way was not required; instead, the resolution language was amended to prohibit any structure or impediment within that right-of-way.
  • The board gave early feedback on a sketch plan for an industrial subdivision of the former Suffolk Cement sand mine site in Calverton, asking the applicant to return with alternative sketch plans.
  • Senior planner presented a proposed Town Code amendment to reduce the site plan review process from three steps to two, eliminating the preliminary approval stage; the board will transmit comments to the Town Board.
