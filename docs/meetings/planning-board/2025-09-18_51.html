Summary AI

The Riverhead Planning Board held three public hearings on minor subdivision applications and approved two resolutions at its September 18, 2025 meeting.

Key actions

  • The board closed the public hearing for a minor subdivision on Roanoke Avenue (Woodhull at South Shore), where a family-owned property in the RA40 zone is proposed to be split into two lots each exceeding 70,000 square feet for estate planning purposes.
  • The board closed the public hearing for the Thomas Anderson Minor Subdivision, which would split a roughly 25-acre parcel on the east side of Roanoke Avenue into two lots, with the larger portion to be sold to Suffolk County for preservation as farmland.
  • The board closed the public hearing for the Richard Anderson Minor Subdivision, a related application to split a roughly 67-acre parcel into three lots, with the larger portions similarly intended for county preservation; the town water district has expressed interest in purchasing a four-acre retained parcel.
  • The board approved a resolution issuing a negative declaration for the BJ's Fueling Station and Wholesale Club expansion (Resolution 2025-094).
  • The board granted final site plan approval for a clubhouse structure at Sandy Pond Links LLC (Resolution 2025-095).
