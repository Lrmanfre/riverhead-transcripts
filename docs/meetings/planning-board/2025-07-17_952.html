Summary AI

The Riverhead Planning Board held its July 17 meeting, advancing several subdivision and site plan applications to public hearings or preliminary approval, and passing nine resolutions.

Key actions

  • The board granted preliminary site plan approval for Sandy Pond's proposed clubhouse, three trade shop buildings, and a manager's residence (Resolution 71), with conditions including relocation of a drainage easement and submission of draft covenants before final approval.
  • The board accepted a revised Draft Environmental Impact Statement for the TJOC Calverton major subdivision/site plan and scheduled a combined public hearing for the first September meeting (Resolutions 72 and 73).
  • The board approved an amended fencing layout around the recharge basin for the Seacrest Estates major subdivision (Resolution 74).
  • An additional resolution related to Seacrest Estates (formerly Villas at Roanoke) approving an amendment to a 2019 approval was passed (Resolution 75).
  • The board classified the 2 Bluffs Drive three-lot minor subdivision as a Type 2 SEQRA action and scheduled a public hearing for August 21 (Resolution 76).
  • The board granted administrative site plan approval for Polish Hall parking lot improvements at 214 Marcy Avenue, including drainage, striping, and a consolidated Sweezy Avenue curb cut, with a condition added requiring dark-sky-compliant lighting (Resolution 77).
  • The board classified the Peconic Farms LLC four-lot minor subdivision on Peconic Bay Boulevard, Jamesport, as a Type 2 SEQRA action and scheduled a public hearing for August 7 (Resolution 78).
  • The board retained a traffic consultant for the Summerwind Farms LLC major subdivision traffic engineering assessment (Resolution 79).

Public hearings & comment

  • A public hearing on 2 Bluffs Drive minor subdivision (three lots from a 3.5-acre vacant parcel served by an existing cul-de-sac) was scheduled for August 21; outstanding questions include the purpose of a proposed right-of-way and the nature of large tanks observed on the property.
  • A public hearing on the Peconic Farms LLC four-lot minor subdivision was scheduled for August 7; staff recommended the lots be restricted from further subdivision.
  • A combined public hearing on the TJOC DEIS, site plan, and subdivision was scheduled for September 4, with a minimum 30-day public comment period on the draft EIS.

Discussed

  • For the 2 Bluffs Drive subdivision, staff noted outstanding items including stormwater details for the common driveway, an erosion and sediment control plan, Health Department comments, a fire marshal request for a possible additional hydrant, and unidentified large tanks visible in aerial photos.
  • The Polish Hall parking lot improvement is linked to a separate application at 505 Lincoln Street, which would use a portion of the lot under a shared parking agreement still requiring Zoning Board of Appeals approval.
