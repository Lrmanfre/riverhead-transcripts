Summary AI

The Riverhead Planning Board held its January 2, 2025 meeting, closing a public hearing on a minor subdivision in Calverton and granting conditional approval to a five-lot subdivision on Union/Maple Avenue.

Public hearings & comment

  • The board closed the public hearing for the Prisco's Calverton Acres application, which seeks to split a 2.06-acre parcel at 3674 Middle Country Road into two lots; the Zoning Board previously granted three variances for lot width and impervious surface coverage.

Key actions

  • The board approved Resolution 25-001, granting conditional approval of the RH Hamptons LLC five-lot subdivision between Maple and Union Avenues, as amended to include highway department requirements for curbing and sidewalks.
  • The board approved the November 7th meeting minutes with a corrected title page.
