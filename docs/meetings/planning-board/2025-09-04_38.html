September 4, 2025 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page

Summary AI

The Planning Board closed a public hearing on the TJOC Real Estate Holdings subdivision draft EIS, accepted a supplemental draft EIS for the Calverton Industrial Subdivision (OSTAT), and approved several resolutions including scheduling a public hearing on OSTAT for November 6, 2025.

Key actions

  • The board voted to close the public hearing on the TJOC Real Estate Holdings application, which covers a draft environmental impact statement for a subdivision and site plan.
  • The board accepted the supplemental draft environmental impact statement for the Calverton Industrial Subdivision (OSTAT), an approximately 131-acre major industrial subdivision west of Splish Splash.
  • The board approved a resolution scheduling a public hearing on the Calverton Industrial Subdivision major subdivision application for the first meeting in November, expected to be November 6, 2025 at 6 p.m.
  • The board approved a resolution selecting a preferred sketch plan (Plan 6) for the Summerwind Farms Major Subdivision, a proposed residential subdivision on approximately 30 acres.
  • A resolution related to an application at Anza Square to construct a commercial center was approved by a 4-1 vote.
  • The board discussed the RH Hampton 321 LLC unmerger request for two lots at 321 Union Avenue and 314 Maple Avenue; staff noted the lots do not meet a required size condition, so the applicant must seek Zoning Board of Appeals relief before the Planning Board can act.
  • The board approved August 7, 2025 meeting minutes.
