Summary AI

The Riverhead Planning Board held three public hearings and approved several resolutions at its June 5 meeting, including actions on a cannabis dispensary site plan, a golf course clubhouse, and various subdivision and site plan matters.

Key actions

  • The public hearing for Gouda LLC was closed; the application seeks approval for a roughly 1,359-square-foot office building at 365 Harrison Avenue, with previously granted ZBA variances for a reduced side yard setback and buffer.
  • The public hearing for Sandy Pond Links LLC was closed; the application proposes a clubhouse, property manager residence, and trade shop buildings at 1521 Roanoke Avenue accessory to an existing golf course.
  • The public hearing for a cannabis dispensary at 840 Old Country Road was closed; the site plan involves reconfiguring parking and a curb cut for a change of use to an adult-use retail cannabis dispensary in the Business Center zone.
  • The board voted to deem the draft environmental impact statement for a parcel on Middle Country Road (TJOC Real Estate Holdings) inadequate.
  • The board issued a positive declaration requiring a supplemental environmental impact statement for the Calvertine Industrial Subdivision.
  • Administrative site plan approval was granted for National Grid facility upgrades.
  • Extension approvals were granted for the Susan McCarthy minor subdivision and the Manna Lane minor subdivision; two 90-day extensions were also granted for the Malise Property minor subdivision.
  • Administrative site plan approval was granted for facade improvements at 1080–1120 Old Country Road, covering painting of both buildings, code-compliant lighting, and added landscaping and trash receptacles.

Public hearings & comment

  • A resident near the 840 Old Country Road cannabis dispensary application spoke in opposition, citing proximity to senior communities, a children's ranch, and a hospital, and raising traffic safety concerns at the Ostrander Avenue corner.

Discussed

  • A Chapter 219 coastal erosion application for a property at 2705 Roanoke Avenue was introduced; the proposal is for a small studio and detached garage seaward of the Coastal Erosion Hazard Area line, and the applicant must first obtain ZBA relief before returning to the Planning Board.
  • The board noted the 840 Old Country Road cannabis site has already received location approval from the town and is on a commercial corridor where the 1,000-foot residential buffer requirement does not apply.
  • The next Planning Board meeting is scheduled for July 3, 2025, at 6 p.m., as the board will not meet on June 19.
