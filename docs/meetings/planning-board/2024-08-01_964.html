August 1, 2024 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Riverhead Planning Board advanced three applications at its August 1 meeting, including a minor subdivision on West Main Street, a tabled subdivision pending ZBA action, and a new restaurant site plan that will go to public hearing in September.

Key actions

  • The board approved a SEQRA classification resolution for the Calverton Acres minor subdivision, designating the Planning Board as lead agency.
  • A second Calverton Acres resolution was tabled pending required Zoning Board of Appeals determinations.
  • The board passed a SEQRA classification resolution for a proposed two-lot minor subdivision at 230 West Main Street, a 14,557 sq ft parcel in the DC-3 zoning district; the applicant must obtain ZBA approval for undersized lot widths before the Planning Board can hold a public hearing.
  • The board classified a SEQRA action and scheduled a public hearing for the Atrium Cafe site plan at 206 Edgar Avenue, a proposed 2,382 sq ft restaurant with 34 indoor and 8 outdoor seats; the public hearing is set for the first September meeting.

Discussed

  • Staff recommended future cross-access easements and a shared parking agreement for the two proposed lots at 230 West Main Street to allow for orderly future development without multiple curb cuts.
  • For the Atrium Cafe, staff flagged the need for a sidewalk along the parcel frontage to connect an existing crosswalk from the elementary school across Edgar Avenue, and noted that Pine Barrens sanitary credits will be required before approval.
  • A board member raised a question about proximity of the Atrium Cafe to the school and the potential effect on a liquor license; staff indicated a beer, wine, and cider license would not be subject to the same restrictions as a full liquor license.
  • The next Planning Board meeting is scheduled for Thursday, August 15 at 3 p.m.
