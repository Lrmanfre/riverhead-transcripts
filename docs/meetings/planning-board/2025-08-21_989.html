August 21, 2025 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting

Summary AI

The Planning Board scheduled public hearings for three minor subdivision applications and granted approvals for several pending applications, including a preliminary site plan for a West Main Street redevelopment and final approval for Gouda LLC.

Key actions

  • The board approved a resolution classifying the Woodhull at Sound Avenue two-lot minor subdivision (506 Townshore Road) as a Type 2 action and scheduled a public hearing for September 18.
  • The board granted final site plan approval for Gouda LLC.
  • The board granted preliminary site plan approval, as amended, for the 940–946 West Main Street site redevelopment; the approval is valid for one year with the possibility of two six-month extensions.
  • The board approved an extension of approval for the Silverback Holdings LLC two-lot minor subdivision.
  • The board classified the Thomas Anderson and Richard Anderson minor subdivisions as Type 2 actions and scheduled public hearings for both on September 18.
  • The board approved an extension of approval for the Kathy Lane 10-lot major subdivision.
  • The next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, September 4 at 6 p.m.

Discussed

  • For the Woodhull subdivision, staff noted that two accessory sheds are in non-conforming locations and will need to either be relocated or receive zoning relief, and a $5,000 recreational fee applies to the new lot.
  • The JPD Calverton site plan at 4195 Middle Country Road was reviewed for status only; the application was previously paused under a Calverton industrial moratorium and the traffic study is ongoing, with no board action taken.
  • The board reviewed three revised sketch plans for the Summerwind Farms major subdivision; staff and the town's consulting engineer recommended sketch plan 6, which locates a private driveway at the southeast corner of the property on Peconic Bay Boulevard, and the board was asked to indicate a preference individually rather than by formal vote.
