Summary AI

The Planning Board met briefly on January 4, 2024, adopting several resolutions including accepting the Draft Environmental Impact Statement for the Calverton Industrial Subdivision, while noting a town board moratorium on Calverton industrial development will pause further in-house review once filed with the Secretary of State.

Key actions

  • The board accepted the Draft EIS for the Calverton Industrial Subdivision and authorized its circulation to involved agencies and public posting, with in-house review to pause once a six-month town moratorium on Calverton industrial development takes effect.
  • The board classified the Delalio Sod Farm action under SEQRA, assumed lead agency, and issued a negative declaration.
  • The board classified the Theodora Cohen minor subdivision under SEQRA and scheduled a public hearing for the application to subdivide two contiguous improved parcels.
