October 17, 2024 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Riverhead Planning Board held public hearings on two site plan applications, approved several resolutions including a site plan amendment for Kent Animal Shelter, issued a positive declaration requiring an Environmental Impact Statement for a mixed-use Calverton development, and discussed a residential rezoning referral on Middle Road.

Key actions

  • The board closed the public hearing for an 8,775-square-foot medical office building proposed for Riverhead Commerce Park on Commerce Drive and discussed conditions including a potential one-way cross-access easement to the adjacent commercial property and a sidewalk requirement.
  • The board closed the public hearing and approved (Resolution 2024-072) the site plan for Project 58, a conversion of a vacant 5,233-square-foot building at 1199 Old Country Road into a 56-seat restaurant.
  • The board approved (Resolution 2024-080) an amendment to the Kent Animal Shelter site plan to reflect building renovations, a new on-site fire hydrant, and updated utility locations, subject to Architectural Review Board approval of revised elevations and water district requirements.
  • The board assumed lead agency status and issued a positive declaration (Resolution 2024-081) for a proposed Calverton development by TGOC Real Estate Holdings, requiring a formal Environmental Impact Statement; a public scoping session will be held.
  • The board approved a negative declaration and scheduled a public hearing for a Riverhead Mazda site plan application (Resolutions 2024-077 and 2024-078).
  • The board approved minor subdivision extensions or approvals for Delalio Sod Farm LLC (Resolution 2024-079), Manor Lane Subdivision (Resolution 2024-082), and JETS 139 LLC (Resolution 2024-083).

Discussed

  • Staff presented a change-of-zone referral for a property at 408 Middle Road seeking rezoning from Retirement Community to Residence A-40 to allow one legal accessory apartment; the board indicated general support and staff will draft a recommendation resolution to transmit to the Town Board, which is awaiting resolution of existing justice court violations before acting.
  • For the TGOC Calverton project, staff noted concerns about potential impacts to wildlife habitat, archaeological resources, and traffic on Middle Country Road as the basis for the positive declaration; limited clearing on the site for archaeological survey purposes was acknowledged as permissible under a code exemption.
