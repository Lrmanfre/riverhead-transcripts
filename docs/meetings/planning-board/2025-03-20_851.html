March 20, 2025 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Planning Board approved a farm stand, advanced a four-lot minor subdivision using transfer of development rights, assumed lead agency and issued a negative declaration for a West Main Street self-storage redevelopment, and rejected a sketch plan for the Summerwind Farm major subdivision after documents were found to have been misrepresented as prepared by a licensed design professional.

Key actions

  • Resolution 2025-022 was approved, granting preliminary site plan approval for a property at 1107 Old Country Road.
  • Resolution 2025-023 was approved, classifying the action under SEQRA and granting farm stand approval for Freeboard 23 LLC for a seasonal, movable farm stand truck; approval is subject to DOT and Suffolk County Farmland Committee review.
  • Resolution 2025-024 was approved, scheduling a public hearing on the Points East LLC minor subdivision application to subdivide a 2.05-acre parcel into four lots using town Transfer of Development Rights.
  • Resolution 2025-025 was approved, assuming lead agency status and issuing a negative declaration for the Leesam Associates LLC minor subdivision, which follows the zoning district boundary between the rural corridor and agricultural protection zones.
  • Resolution 2025-026 was approved, assuming lead agency status and issuing a negative declaration for the 940–946 West Main Street redevelopment, a proposed self-storage and light-industrial tenant building project; a prior motion to table the resolution over soil contamination concerns was also made and seconded but the main resolution ultimately passed.
  • Resolution 2025-027 was approved, rejecting the sketch plan for the Summerwind Farm major subdivision after documents purportedly prepared by a licensed design professional were found not to have been; the board directed all related matters to the town attorney's office.
  • Minutes of the February 20 and 21 meetings were approved.
  • The next Planning Board meeting is scheduled for Thursday, April 3, 2025, at 6 p.m.

Discussed

  • The Points East LLC subdivision would require purchase of two TDR credits, an amended map showing driveway turnaround, drainage, elevations, and water main extension, and a common driveway easement with engineered grading plans before or at the public hearing.
  • Board members raised concerns about potential soil contamination from vehicles stored on the West Main Street site and requested supplemental soil sampling concurrent with the SEQRA process; the applicant noted a prior Phase 2 environmental study came back clean.
