Summary AI

The Riverhead Planning Board discussed a minor subdivision and tabled a farm stand application pending more details, while approving a coastal erosion permit and an amendment related to a separate development's environmental review.

Key actions

  • The board approved resolution 2026-072 granting a Chapter 219 Coastal Erosion Permit for a cottage addition at the Sound Shore Club seasonal community in Wading River.
  • The board tabled resolution 2026-073 for the LCT Vineyards 2026 Farm Stand Approval after members raised concerns about unresolved plumbing, electric, solar, and property-line/fence encroachment issues; the applicant was asked to return in two weeks with revised plans.
  • The board approved resolution 2026-074, amending an earlier resolution (2026-069) concerning a DEIS inadequacy determination for the Breezy Hill Group VI, LLC project.
  • The board discussed but took no formal action on a proposed minor subdivision for the Heidi Magnussen Revocable Trust property on Aliperti Road/Main Road in Jamesport, pending further input from the applicant's representative.

Discussed

  • For the LCT Vineyards farm stand on Union Avenue in Aquebogue, the applicant described plans for a seasonal, weekends-only outdoor farm stand using an existing barn, selling grapes, wine made from the vineyard's grapes, and other local products like native plants and baked goods, with no on-site alcohol consumption.
  • Planning staff noted a highway department requirement for a permanent apron at the Union Avenue access point and identified that an existing gravel road and a neighbor's fence appear to encroach onto the applicant's flag lot, requiring a survey correction.
  • Board members raised concerns about handicap-accessible parking placement on a sloped site and requested the plan show a walking path, possible terracing, and the location of a portable toilet.
  • A board member provided an update on code violations and a stop-work order at an unspecified property, noting fines and permits have been resolved and that maps were signed allowing work to resume after county filing.
