The Riverhead Planning Board approved four resolutions at its December 19 meeting, covering a storage facility, a road project, a restaurant, and an environmental scoping action.

Key actions

  • The board granted final site plan approval for Extra Space Storage (resolution 2024-096).
  • The board approved a resolution related to a Marsden Road matter (resolution 2024-097).
  • The board adopted a final scope for a Draft Environmental Impact Statement for TJOC Real Estate Holding LLC under SEQRA (resolution 2024-098).
  • The board granted administrative site plan approval for Cuddy's Restaurant and Pub (resolution 2024-099).
