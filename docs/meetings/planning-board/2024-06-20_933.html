June 20, 2024 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting

Summary AI

The Planning Board discussed three applications and approved two resolutions, covering a major subdivision in Jamesport, a BP gas station facade update, and a long-running retail development proposal in Wading River. The board also voted to recommend the town board consider a one-way street designation on East 2nd Street.

Key actions

  • The board approved an administrative site plan for a BP gas station facade and pavement maintenance, subject to Architectural Review Board sign-off, with all prior approval conditions remaining in place.
  • The board approved a resolution classifying the Carson Woods Major Subdivision as an unlisted action under SEQRA and authorizing staff to circulate a lead agency request to involved agencies.
  • The board approved a resolution transmitting a letter to the town board recommending consideration of a one-way designation on a portion of East 2nd Street, with specific block language omitted so the Traffic Safety Committee can evaluate the full corridor.

Public hearings & comment

  • There were no public hearings scheduled for this meeting.
  • A Wading River Civic Association representative expressed concern that the proposed Venezia Square development and associated traffic signal could worsen congestion on Route 25A.
  • A resident of Dogwood Drive raised concerns about pedestrian and bicycle safety near the proposed Venezia Square driveway, citing the adjacent rails-to-trails path and a nearby church.

Discussed

  • Staff presented the preliminary plat for Carson Woods, an eight-lot clustered residential subdivision on a 20-acre wooded parcel in Jamesport, noting SEQRA review must address potential northern long-eared bat habitat and proximity to an archaeologically sensitive area.
  • The board discussed requiring a split-rail fence along a 50-foot open space buffer on the eastern property line of Carson Woods and requiring deed covenants acknowledging the adjacent agricultural operation.
  • The open space parcel of roughly 12.8 acres at Carson Woods must eventually be designated as town-dedicated, conservation organization, or homeowner association property before final approval.
  • The Venezia Square retail project on Route 25A in Wading River was reviewed for traffic; a traffic consultant confirmed outstanding comments have been resolved and conditioned any approval on installation of a traffic signal opposite Dogwood Drive, pending New York State DOT permitting.
  • Staff noted the Venezia Square application still requires a water district map and plan and a Pine Barrens Commission determination before a SEQRA determination and public hearing can be scheduled.
  • The board noted there will be no meeting on July 4th; the next meeting is scheduled for July 18th.
