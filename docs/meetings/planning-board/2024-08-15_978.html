August 15, 2024 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Planning Board held a public hearing on a minor subdivision in Jamesport and advanced a Qdoba drive-through restaurant conversion on Old Country Road, scheduling both a SEQRA negative declaration and a public hearing for that project.

Key actions

  • The board closed the public hearing for the Manor Lane Minor Subdivision (JETS 139 LLC), which proposes splitting a 5.037-acre parcel at 139 Sound Avenue, Jamesport into two lots of approximately 2.4 and 2.6 acres; a nearby 19.67-acre parcel with development rights sold to Suffolk County is being incorporated into the map.
  • The board adopted a resolution classifying the Qdoba drive-through restaurant conversion at 949 Old Country Road as an unlisted SEQRA action and issued a negative declaration.
  • The board adopted a resolution scheduling a public hearing on the Qdoba site plan application for September 5, 2024.
  • The board adopted a resolution granting a six-month extension of preliminary site plan approval for HK Ventures LLC.
  • The board approved the minutes of the July 18 meeting.
  • The next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, September 5, 2024 at 6 p.m.

Discussed

  • The Qdoba project proposes converting the former Friendly's restaurant at 949 Old Country Road into two tenancies: a Qdoba drive-through with indoor and outdoor seating, and an unspecified sit-down restaurant, with parking lot reconfiguration, facade renovations, new lighting, and landscaping improvements.
  • Board members raised questions about drive-through queuing capacity, pedestrian crosswalk safety across the drive-through lane, accessible parking placement, loading and delivery logistics, and the condition of fencing on adjacent property.
  • Staff noted the site is within the Riverhead Sewer District and sewer connection is available; health department review is ongoing.
  • The project is scheduled for Architectural Review Board review before the September public hearing.
