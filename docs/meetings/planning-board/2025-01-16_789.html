Summary AI

The Riverhead Planning Board approved six resolutions at its January 16 meeting, advancing two final subdivision plats, one minor subdivision, one administrative site plan, one preliminary site plan, and a performance bond reduction for an ongoing subdivision project.

Key actions

  • The board approved a minor subdivision for Prisco's Calverton Acres, a two-lot split in Calverton, conditioned on DOT requirements including a shared curb cut and common driveway easement being satisfied before plan signature.
  • The board granted administrative site plan approval to Leo K. Group for 522–526 Pulaski Street in Polish Town, legalizing fire-damage repairs, facade changes, and site alterations, with conditions including an ADA-accessible parking stall, trash enclosure, soil assessment for the stone-blend parking area, dark-sky-compliant lighting documentation, and additional landscaping.
  • The board granted preliminary site plan approval for a medical office building proposed by Seven Waves LLC on the last vacant parcel in the Commerce Drive subdivision, dropping a sidewalk requirement given no sidewalks exist elsewhere in that subdivision, but requiring the applicant to show a future cross-access location on plans.
  • The board approved the final plat for a four-lot minor industrial subdivision for Calverton Industrial Park LLC, with a condition that no site plan applications will be accepted until easement and covenant language is recorded following map filing.
  • The board approved the final plat for Kathy Lane LLC (formerly Northwalk Developers), a 10-lot residential subdivision off Kathy Lane, subject to a performance bond for roadway improvements, park and recreation fees of $5,000 per lot, an excavation permit fee, and recording of covenants before any building permits issue.
  • The board voted to recommend a reduction of the Overlook Homes subdivision performance bond from $283,000 to $214,000 based on a consulting engineer's inspection confirming significant roadway construction has been completed.

Public hearings & comment

  • A member of the public announced that the Suffolk County Historical Society in Riverhead is developing a Grumman exhibit and is seeking artifacts, photographs, and memorabilia from former employees and their families; interested parties were directed to contact the Historical Society's executive director.
