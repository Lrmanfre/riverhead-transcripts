The Planning Board discussed two major development applications and passed four resolutions, including final approval for the Sandy Pond Mini Golf course site plan.

Key actions

  • The board voted to approve a resolution for a site plan application referred to as Extra Space in Riverhead (Resolution 2024-26).
  • The board voted to grant final site plan approval for the Sandy Pond Mini Golf course (Resolution 2024-27), with conditions including recorded covenants restricting hours of operation to 10 p.m. and formalization of a drainage easement.
  • The board voted to grant a six-month extension of preliminary site plan approval for the Long Island Color Flower Association Commercial Center (Resolution 2024-028).
  • A fourth resolution was passed unanimously but the project name was not clearly identified in the transcript.
  • The board voted 3-2 to allow the use of pervious pavers for the West Main Street redevelopment application, enabling the application to proceed to the next stage.

Discussed

  • A preliminary site plan for redevelopment of a roughly 5.9-acre site on West Main Street proposes a three-story self-storage building and a one-story tenant rental building; the application requires three zoning variances including excess floor area ratio, excess impervious surface coverage, and insufficient landscaped area.
  • Staff noted illegal clearing and an unpermitted driveway on the West Main Street site, resulting in a doubled site plan fee, and flagged that an accurate full Environmental Assessment Form must be submitted before the board can classify the action under SEQRA.
  • An unnamed site plan application was reviewed for pre-final conditions including DOT approval, stormwater approval, drainage revisions, a sidewalk along Route 25, three loading spaces, right-turn-only egress, cross-access easements, and updated landscaping with native species.
