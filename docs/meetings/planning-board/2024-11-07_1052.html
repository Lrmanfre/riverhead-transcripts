November 7, 2024 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting

Summary AI

The Planning Board held public hearings on two lot divisions, a new Mazda dealership, and an eight-lot subdivision called Carson Woods; it also acted on five resolutions covering subdivision extensions, site plan approvals, and scheduling future hearings.

Key actions

  • The board closed a public hearing on a two-lot minor subdivision on Soundshore Road and Bayberry Lane, where a zoning variance had already been granted.
  • The board closed a public hearing on a site plan for Riverhead Mazda proposing a roughly 22,000-square-foot dealership with parking and landscaping at 1423 Old Country Road.
  • The board closed a public hearing on Carson Woods Major Subdivision, an eight-lot cluster subdivision on about 20 acres accessible via Fox Lane, preserving roughly 64 percent of the parcel as open space.
  • The board voted to schedule a public scoping hearing on the TJOC real estate mixed-use development environmental impact statement for the December 5, 2024 meeting.
  • The board approved a final extension resolution for Holly Estates minor subdivision, a two-new-lot subdivision that has been before the board since 2009, finding all conditions satisfied.
  • The board granted administrative site plan approval for CAV 896 LLC to convert about 3,200 square feet at an oncology center, with a recorded covenant restricting future use changes without new site plan review.
  • The board voted to schedule a public hearing on the 230 West Main Street minor subdivision, which received lot-width variances from the Zoning Board of Appeals in September 2024.

Public hearings & comment

  • Residents on Fox Lane raised concerns about traffic, loss of the cul-de-sac, noise, and privacy, and the planner noted the road extension was planned as far back as 1990.
  • An adjacent orchard owner asked for a western buffer between the Carson Woods lots and his active farming operation, citing pesticide spray; board members indicated a 25-foot buffer would be considered.
  • A Fox Lane resident raised concerns about a fire hydrant proposed near a mature oak tree, and the planner offered to discuss relocating it with the applicant and water district.
  • The Chapter 219 coastal erosion application for a property on Soundshore Road was discussed as a staff item; the applicant must obtain Zoning Board of Appeals relief and may need a wetland determination before returning to the Planning Board.

Discussed

  • For the TJOC EIS scoping, the board noted it wants to see an alternative building orientation studied and intends to remove any presumptive language from the draft scope before adopting a final scope.
  • The planner clarified that the 230 West Main Street parcels fall within a public parking district, removing any requirement for on-site parking for future redevelopment.
