April 3, 2025 — Planning Board

Summary AI

The Riverhead Planning Board held a public hearing on a proposed Islamic place of worship and burial ground on Route 25A in Wading River, then approved three resolutions including final site plan approval for Riverhead Mazda.

Public hearings & comment

  • The board held a public hearing on the Cox Place of Worship site plan, which proposes converting an existing single-family dwelling into a chapel and adding an approximately 3,600-plot burial ground on a 4.98-acre parcel at 5284 Route 25A in Wading River within the RB80 zoning district.
  • The applicant's attorney stated a negative declaration was issued under environmental review and described planned buffers including roughly 374 trees and evergreens around the burial site.
  • The applicant stated the chapel is intended for funeral services only, with a capacity determined by the fire marshal and anticipated at no more than 20 people, served by 14 parking spaces.
  • A neighboring resident raised concerns about traffic, the frequency of visitors, and the potential for future expansion; board staff explained site plan review does not involve land-use policy decisions and that any future expansion would require a new site plan approval.
  • The public hearing was closed by board vote.

Key actions

  • The board approved a two-lot minor subdivision for Lizard & Associates (Resolution 2025-028).
  • The board granted final site plan approval for Riverhead Mazda (Resolution 2025-029).
  • The board approved two 90-day extensions for a minor subdivision approval for Vincent Franchini (Resolution 2025-030).
  • The board adopted the amended minutes of the March 6, 2025 meeting, which included a correction to the Summerwind discussion.
  • The next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, April 17, 2025 at 3 p.m.
