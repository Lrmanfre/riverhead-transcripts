May 2, 2024 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Planning Board held a brief meeting with no public hearings, approving two resolutions and discussing a downtown parking and traffic concept.

Key actions

  • The board scheduled a public hearing for June 6 on the Vincent Franchini Minor Subdivision, a proposed two-lot split at 196 South Jamesport Avenue in Jamesport (ZBA relief for lot size was previously granted).
  • The board approved a coastal erosion hazard area permit for in-kind replacement of an existing 55-foot wooden retaining wall at 84 Cliff Road, East Quogue River.
  • Staff discussed a concept to make Second and Third Streets one-way to add roughly 15 parking stalls downtown, and agreed to research the idea further before any transmittal to the Town Board.
