October 3, 2024 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Planning Board took action on three resolutions and briefly discussed two subdivision applications at its October 3 meeting. No public hearings were held.

Key actions

  • The board approved a resolution assuming lead agency status and issuing a negative declaration for Carson Woods, an eight-lot major residential subdivision in Jamesport.
  • The board scheduled a public hearing for the Carson Woods major subdivision, to be located at the terminus of two lanes in Jamesport.
  • The board scheduled a public hearing for a minor subdivision application for a property on Sound Shore Road, noting the applicant had already obtained required Zoning Board relief.

Discussed

  • A five-lot subdivision on Maple and Union Avenue (RH Hamptons, LLC) was discussed informally; the board is awaiting a revised plan addressing Long Island Workforce Housing Act compliance before taking action.
