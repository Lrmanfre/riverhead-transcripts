February 6, 2025 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting

Summary AI

The Planning Board advanced several applications at its February 6 meeting, approving four resolutions including a coastal erosion permit, a SEQRA classification and public hearing date for a medical office, a traffic consultant retention for a long-running retail project, and a 19-lot subdivision yield map. A proposed place of worship and churchyard on Route 25A was discussed but requires additional environmental review before a public hearing can be scheduled.

Key actions

  • The board approved a coastal erosion hazard permit application for a beach access stairway at 118 Northside Road, Wading River, with revegetation and construction detail conditions noted.
  • The board classified a 2,290 sq ft medical office application at 1125 Old Country Road as a Type 2 SEQRA action and scheduled a public hearing for the first Planning Board meeting in March.
  • The board voted to retain a traffic consultant for the Eastside Square multi-building retail project near the town line, with costs paid by the applicant.
  • The board approved a 19-lot as-of-right subdivision yield map for the Summerwind Farms Major Subdivision, a roughly 30-acre split-zoned parcel at 200 Peconic Bay Boulevard.
  • SEQRA coordination was initiated for a subdivision application by Lives of Associates LLC, starting a 30-day agency coordination period.

Public hearings & comment

  • No public comments were recorded on any matter before the board.
  • The Cox Place of Worship and churchyard application on Route 25A was discussed only; a full Environmental Assessment Form addressing groundwater impacts is required before a public hearing can be set.

Discussed

  • The Cox Place of Worship proposal would convert a single-family dwelling and add a 3,600-plot private churchyard; the site relies on a private well, with the nearest public water main approximately 3,500 feet away at an estimated extension cost of $350–$400 per linear foot.
  • The board flagged a potential impervious surface coverage issue for the Cox application, as combined calculations may exceed the RB zone's 15% maximum, and requested clarification from the applicant's engineer.
  • For Eastside Square, the state DOT has conceptually agreed to a proposed traffic signal but is requiring left-turn lanes and retention of the existing bike lane; the board needs a conceptual sketch of all DOT-required improvements before making a significance determination.
  • The Summerwind Farms sketch plans were reviewed; one layout raised concerns about sight distance, elimination of a wooded buffer, and five lots with direct access onto Peconic Bay Boulevard.
  • The board discussed recommending to the Town Board that a volunteer committee be formed to help report dark sky and lighting violations, easing the burden on code enforcement, which currently has an officer available for nighttime inspections.
