September 19, 2024 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Planning Board scheduled two public hearings, tabled one site plan resolution, and approved several extensions and modifications at its September 19 meeting.

Key actions

  • The board voted to table a pending site plan resolution for a restaurant conversion at 1199 Old Country Road (referred to as 'Project 58') rather than approve it outright.
  • The board approved a resolution scheduling a public hearing for the Project 58 restaurant conversion site plan application for October 17 at 3:00 p.m.
  • The board approved a resolution scheduling a public hearing on a preliminary site plan application for a one-story medical office building proposed by 27 Waves LLC on Commerce Drive.
  • The board approved a 12-month extension of final site plan approval for the Riverhead Town Home site plan.
  • The board approved a lot line modification extension for one applicant and granted a lot line modification approval for a second applicant.
  • August 15 meeting minutes were approved.

Discussed

  • For the restaurant conversion at 1199 Old Country Road, outstanding items include a soil boring or test pit to confirm groundwater depth beneath proposed drainage areas, a sewer district key money assessment, ARB review, and a dumpster screening detail meeting Town Code fencing requirements.
  • For the Commerce Drive medical office proposal, staff recommended adding a cross-access connection to the adjacent property to the west, installing sidewalks, and completing restriping at the Commerce Drive and Route 58 intersection in coordination with the town DPW.
  • Board members raised questions about shared parking management between the restaurant and an existing office tenant at the 1199 Old Country Road site, and about whether a fire exit door at the rear of the building has adequate vehicle clearance.
