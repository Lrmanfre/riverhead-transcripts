Summary AI

The Planning Board met on February 20, 2025, discussed two projects, and passed several resolutions including classifying a fueling station canopy project and recommending a performance bond reduction for a subdivision.

Key actions

  • The board voted to classify the Bowler Market Company canopy and site improvement application at 4129 Middle Country Road as a Type 2 SEQRA action, allowing the Zoning Board of Appeals to proceed on a required front-yard setback variance.
  • The board approved a resolution recommending a performance bond reduction for Seacrest Estates (formerly the Hills at Roanoke) subdivision to $400,000, to be forwarded to the Town Board.
  • The board also passed resolutions on a Coastal Erosion Hazard Area permit and a letter of recommendation to the Town Board regarding dark-skies lighting standards.
