The Riverhead Planning Board held a brief July 2 meeting, approving four resolutions covering a coastal erosion permit, a site plan amendment, and two subdivision security matters. A previously scheduled discussion item was pulled from the agenda.

Key actions

  • The board approved a Chapter 219 Coastal Erosion Permit for a reduced rear deck replacement at 888 Soundshore Road in Jamesport, subject to a revegetation plan for the bluff top.
  • The board approved a site plan amendment for a business at 720 Main Road in Jamesport, reconfiguring the driveway to a single curb cut with 90-degree parking and updated building materials per New York State DOT requirements.
  • The board approved performance security releases for two subdivisions and voted to adjourn; the next meeting is July 16 at 3 p.m.
