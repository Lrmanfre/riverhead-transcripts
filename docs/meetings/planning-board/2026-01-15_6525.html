Summary AI

The Riverhead Planning Board approved four resolutions at its January 15 meeting, covering a site plan, a performance bond release, a medical center application, a pool site improvement, and a minor subdivision extension.

Key actions

  • The board granted preliminary site plan approval for the application at 305 West Main Street (Resolution 2026-001).
  • The board approved a resolution to release a performance bond for NM Golf.
  • The board approved the Peconic Bay Medical Center Northwell Health maternity relocation and expansion site plan resolution (Resolution 2026-003).
  • The board approved Resolution 2026-004 for Van Bander Pools site improvements as amended, adding a condition requiring the town engineer to confirm the drainage system design is adequate.
  • The board approved two 90-day extensions for a minor subdivision at 230 West Main Street (Resolution 2026-005).
