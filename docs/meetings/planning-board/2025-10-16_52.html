October 16, 2025 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page

Summary AI

The Planning Board approved a site plan for a new horse barn at Timothy Hill Children's Ranch and granted a one-year extension for a final site plan at a separate development site. No public hearings were held.

Key actions

  • The board approved an administrative site plan for Timothy Hill Children's Ranch (298 Middle Road) to build an approximately 6,500-square-foot horse barn, replacing a demolished 4,200-square-foot barn, subject to conditions including stormwater management, health department approval, a town board resolution, and trimming of evergreen trees near a driveway for visibility.
  • The board granted a one-year extension for a final site plan associated with a separate development, referred to in resolution 2025-101, where the originally planned tenant has since withdrawn and no new tenant has been secured.
