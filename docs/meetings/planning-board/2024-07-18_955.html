Summary AI

The Riverhead Planning Board reviewed five active applications at its July 18 meeting, approving negative declarations, scheduling public hearings, and classifying two major projects under environmental review. A proposed townhouse parking workaround was rejected by the board.

Key actions

  • The board classified the Jets 139 LLC minor subdivision as a Type 2 action under SEQRA and scheduled a public hearing (Resolution 2024-46).
  • The board classified a minor subdivision for a property at 86 Second Street in South Jamesport (Mallets property) as an unlisted action and issued a negative declaration, allowing the applicant to proceed to the ZBA for required variances (Resolution 2024-48).
  • The board granted administrative approval to amend a previously approved site plan for a facade improvement project at 1125 Old Country Road, satisfying a prior condition requiring an approved grading and drainage plan (Resolution 2024-49).
  • The board approved amending the Seacrest Estates major subdivision from 14 lots to 13 lots and authorized acceptance of a fee in lieu of the required affordable housing density bonus lot, with funds dedicated to workforce housing purposes (Resolution 2024-051).
  • The board classified a combined site plan and major subdivision application for TJOC State Holdings LLC — a mixed-use development proposed on a 15.8-acre vacant site on Middle Country Road in Calverton — as a Type 1 action under SEQRA, initiating mandatory coordinated review (Resolution 2024-052).
  • The board declined to support adding a front-yard parking stall to the 8-unit townhouse project at 46 East Second Street, directing the applicant to find another solution to meet the required 12-stall minimum.
  • The Diaz lot line modification at 718 Roanoke Avenue was discussed; the board indicated no objections and directed the applicant to provide an updated contract of sale so a public hearing can be scheduled.
