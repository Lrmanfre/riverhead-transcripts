October 1, 2026 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Planning Board discussed restarting the long-dormant RD Pilesgrove (formerly Kapsalis) major subdivision at Sound Avenue from the preliminary stage with a new application fee and SEQRA review, and approved a resolution legalizing improvements at Great Rock Golf Club.

Key actions

  • The board approved Resolution 2026-062, granting administrative site plan approval to legalize clearing and drainage improvements at Great Rock Golf Club in Wading River.
  • The board approved the September 3, 2026 meeting minutes.
  • The chair gave an update that a stop-work order remains in effect on the Summerwind project while the town negotiates with the applicant.

Discussed

  • For the RD Pilesgrove LLC (formerly Kapsalis) subdivision at 3544 Sound Avenue, the applicant's engineers and town planning staff discussed restarting the application at preliminary approval with a new fee, updated SEQRA (coordinated) review, and eventual public hearing, since the original 2008 final conditional approval has lapsed beyond allowable extensions.
  • Staff and board members discussed open space and preservation percentages for the subdivision, noting the plan currently shows about 61% land area preserved versus the town's 70% target, and considered reducing the lot yield to possibly avoid Long Island Workforce Housing Act requirements and a mandatory Type 1 SEQRA classification.
