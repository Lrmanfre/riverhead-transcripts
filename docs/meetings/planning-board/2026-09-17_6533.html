Summary AI

The Planning Board discussed three site plan applications (Hampton Motors, Riverhead Plaza pad sites, and Great Rock golf course improvements) without final votes except one SEQRA resolution, and heard extended public comment challenging a previously approved cannabis-related site plan in Aquebogue.

Key actions

  • The board adopted Resolution 2026-061, classifying the Hampton Motors site plan as an Unlisted Action under SEQRA and issuing a Negative Declaration.
  • No other resolutions were voted on; the Riverhead Plaza pad sites and Great Rock golf course applications remain under discussion pending revisions.

Discussed

  • Hampton Motors (300 Mill Road) plans a building addition and new storage building; staff flagged needed zoning relief for rear and side yard setbacks, a missing landscape and lighting plan, drainage plan, ADA parking, and wetlands review, with the application expected to proceed to the zoning board for relief.
  • Riverhead Plaza pad sites (765 Old Country Road) proposes two new restaurant buildings; the applicant has acquired required transfer of development rights, but the site currently falls short of required parking under town code, and county DPW comments call for a turn lane on Old Country Road that may require removing recently installed street trees and sidewalks.
  • Great Rock Holdings golf course (Wading River) sought to legalize clearing and improvements near hole 17; staff recommended formalizing a mapped, legally described non-disturbance buffer along the existing tree line to protect neighboring residences, with a resolution to be prepared for the next meeting.
  • A resident's attorney presented detailed objections to a previously approved cannabis-related site plan in Aquebogue, alleging jurisdictional and procedural defects (informal review threshold, missing environmental review, lack of public hearing, missing health department and fire marshal compliance) and requested rescission of the approval and a stop-work order.
  • Other residents asked about the status and enforcement of an existing stop-work order on a nearby site and how they would be notified of updates; staff said updates could be provided at future meetings or by contacting town departments.
