February 15, 2024 — Planning Board

Summary AI

The Planning Board held a public hearing on a proposed Chevrolet dealership at 1309 Old Country Road, approved several resolutions including an administrative site plan for Hallockville Museum Farm barn improvements, and rescheduled a public hearing for the Marist Farm subdivision after improper posting.

Public hearings & comment

  • A public hearing was held for a site plan application proposing a new approximately 18,000-square-foot automobile dealership at 1309 Old Country Road on a 2.07-acre parcel in the Business Center zoning district, with parking, landscaping, drainage, and new water and sewer connections.
  • The applicant stated the revised plan requires fewer variances than the version previously approved by the Zoning Board of Appeals and includes an 8-foot vinyl fence and arborvitae buffer along the eastern boundary near residential properties.
  • No members of the public spoke at the hearing, which was then closed by board vote.
  • A scheduled public hearing for the Marist Farm subdivision was not held because the required posting was not completed properly; a new hearing date was set by resolution.

Key actions

  • The board approved an administrative site plan for interior alterations to the Nogles Barn at Hallockville Museum Farm, including bathrooms, a small food-prep area, interior conditioning, a new sanitary system, a water line, and accessible parking stalls.
  • The Hallockville approval was granted with a condition requiring compliance with all applicable town codes not explicitly listed in the resolution, addressing board concerns about event and wedding use on the property.
  • The board approved a resolution granting an extension of a minor subdivision approval in the Firmature application, which had a prior approval dating to 2015.
  • The board approved a six-month extension of preliminary site plan approval for a project identified as a dry-goods manufacturers outlet center.
  • The board approved a resolution transmitting a recommendation to the town board to amend the speed limit along a portion of Roanoke Avenue north of Middlewood Road.
  • The next Planning Board meeting was announced for Thursday, March 7, 2024 at 6 p.m.

Money

  • Hallockville Museum Farm was assessed a water district key-money fee of $3,075.80 in connection with the new water line for the barn project.
