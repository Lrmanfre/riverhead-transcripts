March 7, 2024 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Planning Board closed a public hearing on a residential subdivision near Rolling Woods, approved several site plans and resolutions, and advanced two new applications to public hearing.

Key actions

  • The board voted to close a public hearing on a minor subdivision creating four residential lots near Linda Lane West and Rolling Woods, with the applicant agreeing to a 30-foot buffer along the lot bordering existing neighborhood properties.
  • The board approved a resolution for the Lally Ossard minor subdivision.
  • The board approved site plan approval for Kershaw Recycling of Riverhead, with the board noting a minor amendment to stormwater language reviewed by the town attorney.
  • The board granted administrative approval for Lucero's Restaurant.
  • The board assumed lead agency and issued a negative declaration, then scheduled a public hearing, for the Northville Acres two-lot minor subdivision at 821 Northville Turnpike.
  • The board granted preliminary site plan approval for an automotive dealership at 1309 OCR.
  • The board scheduled a public hearing for a site plan application by Terra Vita Winery to convert an existing vacant single-family residence into additional tasting room and retail wine sales space at 250 Metter Lane.

Public hearings & comment

  • Several residents of Rolling Woods and Linda Lane West spoke at the subdivision hearing, requesting tree preservation and buffers to protect neighborhood character and wildlife.
  • A resident asked about the distance from South Drive to the nearest proposed lot and whether South Drive would ever be extended; staff confirmed no road or residential development is planned on the agricultural lot.
  • Staff clarified that the subdivision reorganizes two existing tax parcels created when development rights were sold to Suffolk County, resulting in four new residential lots and one preserved agricultural lot.

Discussed

  • The Northville Acres application was revised from a three-lot to a two-lot subdivision after the Conservation Advisory Council objected to development intensity near wetlands associated with Sawmill Creek.
  • The Terra Vita Winery conversion was classified as a Type 2 SEQRA action; staff noted requirements including an RPZ from the water district, Health Department approval, and ADA accessibility.
