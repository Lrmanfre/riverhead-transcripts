Summary AI

The Riverhead Planning Board approved five resolutions at its February 1, 2024 meeting, covering a winery pergola, a bluff stabilization permit, a mini golf course, a subdivision extension, and a settlement agreement.

Key actions

  • The board approved an administrative site plan for RGNY Tasting Room at 6045 Sound Avenue in Jamesport to construct a 39-by-63-foot wooden pergola with a seasonal translucent polycarbonate roof over an existing concrete patio.
  • The board classified the 48 Cliffway coastal erosion project in Calverton as a Type 2 action under SEQRA and approved a Chapter 219 Coastal Erosion Management Permit to construct a new 136-linear-foot steel and timber retaining wall, reinforce existing retaining walls, backfill with 300 cubic yards of clean fill, and install a planted berm.
  • The board granted preliminary site plan approval for Sandy Pond Mini Links mini golf course, with conditions including a stormwater pollution prevention plan determination, revised vegetation buffer treatment, drainage easement documentation, and submission of kiosk elevations for ARB review.
  • The board approved an extension of approval for an unnamed two-lot minor subdivision at 818 Ronald Avenue in Calverton.
  • The board authorized the chairman to execute a settlement agreement and mutual release with a local recycling business.
  • The public hearing on a subdivision application identified as the Theodora Cohen minus subdivision was closed.
  • The next meeting was scheduled for Thursday, February 15, 2024 at 3 p.m.
