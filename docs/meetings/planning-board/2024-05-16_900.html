May 16, 2024 — Planning Board

Planning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Planning Board approved two bluff retaining wall replacements on Lewin Drive, a facade improvement for a retail building on Old Country Road, and took a SEQRA action on a townhouse project. The board also discussed a vegetation buffer violation at the Sandy Pond mini golf site.

Key actions

  • The board approved a Chapter 219 application for a property on Lewin Drive to replace a wooden retaining wall with a steel I-beam and wood-lagging wall along a bluff.
  • The board approved a similar Chapter 219 application for the Lewin Hills Property Owners Association parcel directly west, involving the same construction method and tying into the adjacent retaining wall.
  • The board approved an administrative site plan for facade improvements at 1125 Old Country Road, including new stucco exterior, window replacements, parking lot repaving, landscaping, and formalization of cross-access easements; approval was conditioned on a grading and drainage plan prior to any regrading.
  • The board assumed lead agency status and issued a negative declaration under SEQRA for a townhouse project on 2nd Street.

Discussed

  • Planning staff reported that the project sponsor for Sandy Pond mini golf cleared vegetation in a required 20-foot non-disturbance buffer without following the agreed review process; a substantial replacement landscaping plan was submitted and staff intends to bring a formal approval resolution to the first June meeting.
  • Staff said future site plan applications involving existing vegetation will be pressed for detailed tree inventories within non-disturbance buffers to establish a baseline record.
