July 22, 2025 — Town Board

Special Town Board Meeting
88 min  ·  CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board held a special meeting with two public hearings related to the proposed 'Town Square' hotel project at 127 East Main Street: one on designating J. Petrucelli Riverhead Town Square LLC as a qualified and eligible sponsor, and one on transferring approximately 2,700 square feet of parking district property to the developer. No decision was made; both hearings remain open for written comment until August 1, 2025.

Key actions

  • The board held but did not close either public hearing, leaving a ten-day written comment period open through August 1, 2025.
  • The board announced a separate special meeting on July 24, 2025 at 9:30 a.m. to approve a resolution applying for a grant due by July 31.
  • The board indicated a decision on the qualified-and-eligible designation could come as early as the second August board meeting.

Public hearings & comment

  • The applicant's team presented a project budget of approximately $32.7 million, to be funded through a construction loan, developer equity, and a $1 million Restore New York grant already awarded.
  • The proposed development at 127 East Main Street would include a 76-room branded hotel, 12 rooftop condominium units, three ground-floor retail spaces, and a waterfront restaurant, with underground parking for condo residents.
  • The engineer described transferring strips of approximately 14 feet from a property to the west and 10 feet from a property to the east of the current tax lot to accommodate the hotel footprint.
  • The architect stated the hotel and plaza are designed to sit at elevation 13 to address flood mitigation, based on Army Corps of Engineers projections.
  • Several residents raised concerns about loss of parking, IDA tax abatements written into the draft contract, the short ten-day comment window, and the financial disclosures being tied to existing Petrucelli entities rather than the newly formed LLC.
  • A resident and a board member noted that the parcels involved currently generate no property tax revenue because they are town-owned.
  • One commenter asked the board to extend the comment period to 30 days given the volume of material presented for the first time at the hearing.
  • The applicant's attorney stated that no IDA application has been filed and that any such application would be reviewed by a separate board.

Discussed

  • The status of federal RAISE grant funding for the broader Town Square project was raised by a commenter; a board member stated those funds have not been clawed back and are earmarked for streetscapes and a parking garage rather than the hotel.
  • Questions were raised about impacts on East End Arts buildings, which would need to be relocated during construction, and on an existing tenant known as Crafted.
