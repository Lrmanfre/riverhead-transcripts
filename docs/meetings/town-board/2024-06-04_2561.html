Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board met on June 4, 2024, appointing two new full-time police officers, advancing a major sewer district biosolids project bond, and passing more than 40 resolutions covering staffing, grants, road resurfacing, and special events.

Key actions

  • The board unanimously appointed two probationary officers to full-time positions in the Riverhead Police Department, with one officer sworn in by a town council member who is his parent.
  • The board approved a bond authorization amendment for the Riverhead Sewer District biosolids improvement project, now set at $18,650,000, and approved a related public interest order.
  • A public hearing on the sewer district biosolids bond was kept open for written comment until June 14 at 4 p.m.
  • A proposed resolution (556) related to land use was tabled by a 3-2 vote so board members could review it further; it will be discussed at Thursday's work session.
  • The board adopted road resurfacing grant budgets under the CHIPS, Pave NY, and EWR programs for 2024-2025.
  • The board approved a SEQRA negative declaration and lead agency designation for the Meetinghouse Creek Wetland Restoration project.
  • A public hearing was scheduled on a zoning code amendment regarding alternative pervious parking techniques; the board directed staff to add discretionary language before re-noticing, after a speaker argued the current draft would inadvertently push compliant projects into variance proceedings.
  • An emergency bonding resolution (562) to support a grant application for wellhead treatment at Water District Well 5-2A was adopted, with a public hearing set for June 14, 2024.

Public hearings & comment

  • A public hearing on increasing and improving the Riverhead Sewer District was held; staff said the biosolids project is projected to save the town $10.5 million over its lifecycle and reduce solid waste disposal by 80%.
  • A public hearing on a zoning amendment for alternative pervious parking techniques was held and will be re-noticed after staff revises the draft language.
  • A previously scheduled public hearing on special permit provisions for family dwellings was canceled due to incomplete public notice requirements.
  • Two members of the public spoke during open comment urging the board to preserve the Long Island Science Center's downtown location rather than proceed with any eminent domain action.

Money

  • The sewer district biosolids project bond was amended to $18,650,000, up from an earlier $16 million estimate, with staff attributing roughly 75% of the increase to material and labor cost rises since 2019.
  • Staff reported the biosolids project will reduce annual sludge handling costs from $1.2 million to $500,000 and yield net savings of approximately $96,000 per year during the 20-year bond repayment period.
