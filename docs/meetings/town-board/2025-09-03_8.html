September 3, 2025 — Town Board

Town Board Meeting

Summary AI

The board held a lengthy public hearing on a long-neglected unsafe structure at 39 Pye Lane in South Jamesport, ultimately voting to authorize its demolition. A dozen other resolutions were adopted, including one launching a new town website, one amending site-plan violation fines, and one approving the Community Benefit Zoning code amendment.

Key actions

  • The board authorized the demolition, removal, and abatement of the unsafe structure at 39 Pye Lane, Jamesport (tax map 672), with all members voting yes.
  • The board adopted a local law amending Chapter 301 site-plan review penalties, raising the fine floor from zero to $500 and the ceiling from $1,000 to $5,000; the hearing was kept open for written comment until September 14.
  • The board adopted a local law amending the Community Benefit Zoning Use District provisions of the town code (Chapter 301, Article 17A).
  • The board adopted local law amendments to Chapter 231 (Fire Prevention penalties) and Chapter 251 (Noise and Property Maintenance).
  • The board approved hiring a consulting building plans examiner (Brad Hammond) at the stated rate, after amending the resolution to correct the civil-service title from senior building inspector to building plans examiner; one member voted no citing a lower-cost alternative.
  • The board voted 4-1 to appoint a local farmer to the Agricultural Advisory Committee; the dissenting member stated on the record that the individual had repeatedly sought personal economic relief from the committee and used inappropriate language toward a board member.
  • The board approved publishing a request for proposals for a construction manager for a new Riverhead Volunteer Ambulance Corps headquarters building.
  • The board authorized 2025 bow hunting for deer at Enterprise Park in Calverton.
  • A capital project budget was adopted for improvements at Two Bears Park (Resolution 749).

Public hearings & comment

  • The town engineer testified that the structure at 39 Pye Lane is structurally compromised, open to the elements, a fire and wind-debris hazard to neighboring homes, and recommended immediate demolition; the bulkhead on the property was separately noted as severely deteriorated but was not part of the formal unsafe-structure notice.
  • The property owner, participating by video from Germany, stated willingness to hire a demolition contractor and sign a contract, but no contract had been signed as of the hearing date.
  • Neighbors testified the property has been neglected for many years and urged the board not to grant further delays.
  • The site-plan violation fine increase hearing drew no public speakers.

Discussed

  • A resident asked the board to address speeding and vehicles parking in a road easement on Church Lane in Aquebogue; the police chief committed to assigning officers to the area.
