July 7, 2026 — Town Board

Town Board Meeting

Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board adopted more than 40 resolutions covering budget adjustments, personnel, special events, and zoning changes, while opening a public hearing on a proposed no-parking zone at Sound Avenue and Tooey Avenue, and tabling a docking-law amendment for further discussion.

Key actions

  • A public hearing was opened on a proposed amendment to prohibit parking on both sides of Sound Avenue and Tooey Avenue for 200 feet in each direction from their intersection to improve sight lines; written comments are accepted through July 17.
  • Resolution 666 adopted a local law amending the Business F Zoning Use District, Manufacturers Outlet Center Overlay Zone (Chapter 301, Article 17).
  • Resolution 665, which would have authorized notice of a public hearing to amend the town's docking law (Chapter 293), was tabled for work session discussion.
  • Resolution 668 ratified authorization to issue an RFP for redevelopment of the Railroad Street property as a transit-oriented development.
  • Resolution 645 authorized the purchase of a Meals on Wheels truck for the seniors department.
  • Resolution 640 adjusted the budget for a Suffolk County Water Authority federal pass-through grant to bring clean drinking water to Manorville.
  • Resolutions 641 and 642 adopted budget adjustments to pay down Town Square bonds and a 2018 Series B bond refunding, respectively.
  • Resolution 639 ratified an emergency repair of the East Creek boat launch, which bypassed the standard CAC process.
  • Amy Elias was appointed to the Emerging Technology Committee and Salim Masood was appointed chairman of the Anti-Bias Task Force.
  • Resolutions 670–672 approved special event permits for Chamber of Commerce Concerts in the Park, the Hallockville Hootenanny, and a church press service.
  • Resolution 673 approved a fireworks application for the Jamesport Fire Department for July 18, 2026.

Public hearings & comment

  • Warren McKnight spoke in support of the proposed Sound Avenue and Tooey Avenue parking restrictions.
  • A lifelong resident raised concerns about ICE enforcement near Pulaski Street School and asked the board for a child-safety plan during potential ICE operations at school dismissal time.
  • A speaker criticized board members and the town clerk for alleged rude and disrespectful behavior toward residents.
