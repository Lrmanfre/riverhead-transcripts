Summary AI

The Town Board held a brief special meeting, passing one resolution to accept the Draft Generic Environmental Impact Statement (DGEIS) for the 2024 Comprehensive Plan update and scheduling a public hearing on it.

Key actions

  • The board unanimously adopted a resolution accepting the DGEIS for the 2024 Comprehensive Plan update, declaring it complete for public review purposes, and scheduling a public hearing.

Public hearings & comment

  • A Reeves Park resident asked the board to maintain the current policy restricting beach permits to residents and to preserve the 29-30 day minimum rental rule to curb short-term rental scams and related nuisances.
  • An online commenter sought clarification on when the DGEIS and updated Comprehensive Plan documents would be posted publicly; a planning consultant explained the documents would be available online later that afternoon.
