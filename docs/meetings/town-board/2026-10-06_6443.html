October 6, 2026 — Town Board

Town Board Meeting

Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board promoted two police officers, held public hearings on CDBG funding applications and a traffic code amendment, and advanced routine budget, personnel, and contract resolutions.

Key actions

  • The board adopted resolutions promoting a police officer to Detective 3rd Grade and another to Sergeant, both by 5-0 vote, with the sergeant sworn in during the meeting.
  • The board ratified a budget adjustment for improvements at Two Bears Park (Capital Project #72515) and a Highway Department budget transfer for emergency storm repairs, both adopted 5-0.
  • A public hearing on a local law amending Chapter 289 (Vehicles, Traffic and Parking) to codify an existing stop/yield intersection near the former Halleck Fabricating warehouse was left open until October 16.

Public hearings & comment

  • A public hearing on Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) funding heard presentations from Riverhead CAP, Bread and More, the Warrior Ranch Foundation, and the Riverhead Chamber of Commerce (requesting $7,000 for a youth business mentorship program); public comment on CDBG remains open until October 24.
  • A resident questioned reports that a congressman may seek to cut community development funding.
  • Speakers including a representative of the Greater Calverton Civic Association supported a proposed appraisal of 1458 Middle Road, Calverton, for open space preservation, citing adjacent preserved land and agricultural soils.
  • A resident asked whether prior appraisals of 1458 Middle Road existed; town officials confirmed the town's appraiser would be used and no prior town appraisal had been done, though the purchaser had completed one.

Money

  • Monthly reports showed Building Department revenue of $84,199 (August 2026), Tax Receiver utility payments of $876,951.25 (September 2026), and Town Clerk fees of $11,259.50 (September 2026).

Discussed

  • The board received correspondence on topics including a battery energy storage system (BESS) project, public safety, immigration policy, police detention procedures, ethics, and the 2027 tentative budget.
