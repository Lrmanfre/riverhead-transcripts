October 20, 2026 — Town Board

Town Board Meeting

Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board adopted a large slate of budget amendments, appointments, and contracts, including several split votes on 2027 preliminary budget amendments, and advanced a proposed tax exemption for disabled veterans toward a public hearing; public comments focused on immigration enforcement policy, a storage facility at Sandy Links golf course, and the Riverhead train station's condition.

Key actions

  • The board adopted budget amendments and capital project items for highway drainage, water district wells, and the Youngs Avenue landfill solar project closure, with Councilman Kern credited for work on the solar project.
  • Several 2027 preliminary budget amendments (reducing salt and equipment lines, adding a cost-of-living adjustment and a dental plan for CSEA clerical workers) passed with some board members abstaining over disagreements on budget strategy.
  • The board voted to publish notice of a proposed local law amending Chapter 279 (Taxation) to allow a property tax deferral for 100% disabled veterans, with a public hearing scheduled for the November 5 meeting.
  • A local law amending traffic and parking regulations (Chapter 289) was adopted, and a bid notice for a used Yamaha outboard motor was amended to specify a 2008-or-later model instead of 2018.
  • The board accepted a donated sculpture, 'Grateful Turnout,' honoring first responders for placement in downtown Riverhead, and accepted a donation from a local GM dealer for PAL football and cheerleading.
  • Several appointments were made to the Helicopter Noise Task Force, and a public safety dispatcher was appointed to the police department.
  • The board adopted the final scope for a draft environmental impact statement (DGEIS) on a proposed golf course zoning district and separately accepted the draft DGEIS, setting a public review period and scheduling a SEQRA hearing.

Public hearings & comment

  • Multiple residents urged the board to hold a public work session on town procedures for responding to immigration enforcement actions and to consider adopting a proposed public safety law.
  • A resident questioned a commercial storage facility built at Sandy Links golf course, saying it differed from the 'trade shop' use originally approved; board members said the use was disclosed at a prior work session.
  • A resident raised concerns about the financial standing of the town's selected downtown redevelopment master developer ahead of a planned property sale closing.
  • A resident asked whether the town will update its 2011 fund balance policy and whether the supervisor will forgo a salary increase in 2027; the supervisor said he will not take a salary increase or accept an automatic fringe benefit increase.

Affects residents

  • Residents were reminded of a water system flushing causing temporary discoloration in Wading River tap water, described as safe, and warned about a private mailer offering water testing unaffiliated with the EPA or county health department.
  • A town-wide cleanup day, pumpkin smash, prescription drug take-back event, and other community events are scheduled for upcoming weekends in late October and November.
