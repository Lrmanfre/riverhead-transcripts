May 7, 2024 — Town Board

Town Board Meeting
CivicClerk page

Summary AI

The Town Board held public hearings on a $2 million state Restore NY grant for a downtown hotel project and two unsafe-structure cases, then passed roughly 60 resolutions covering staffing, finances, contracts, and event permits.

Public hearings & comment

  • The board held a public hearing on applying to the NY Empire State Development Restore NY Community Initiative program for a $2 million grant to support a hotel construction and demolition project within the Town Square development; the town would serve as the grant conduit and contribute no funding, with written comments accepted through June 10.
  • A public hearing on a special permit for a two-family dwelling at 4592 Middle Country Road, Calverton was not opened after staff confirmed required mailings were not completed; it will be rescheduled for the next regular meeting.
  • A public hearing on a structure at 650 Elton Street was adjourned to the next meeting after the owner disconnected utilities and applied for a demolition permit.
  • A public hearing on a structure at 1092 Dolores Avenue was cancelled after the property owner removed the structure at their own expense.
  • A representative from the Federation of Organizations spoke during public comment to offer free Narcan training to local businesses and organizations under a state grant.

Key actions

  • The board approved fund balance transfers for bond anticipation note payment, a printer-plotter purchase, and town hall improvements.
  • The board provisionally appointed a code compliance coordinator, a senior accountant, and a Justice Court clerk (Spanish-speaking), while voting down a separate accountant appointment after the candidate withdrew.
  • Two members were appointed to the Anti-Bias Task Force and three members were reappointed to the Veterans Advisory Committee.
  • A member was appointed to the Architectural Review Board.
  • The board ratified the appointment of a municipal garage manager and authorized a stipend, and ratified a separate stipend for an interim garage manager.
  • The board authorized a SEQRA classification and lead agency coordination for the Meeting House Creek Wetlands Restoration project.
  • The board set fees for accelerated building department and fire prevention services.
  • Beach hours were set and seasonal beach and recreation department employees were appointed for 2024.
