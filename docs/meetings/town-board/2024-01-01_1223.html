January 1, 2024 — Town Board

Summary AI

Riverhead held its inauguration ceremony on January 1, 2024, swearing in a new supervisor, two town councilwomen, a town justice, a town clerk, and other officials. The incoming supervisor outlined priorities including EPCAL redevelopment, downtown revitalization, a new ambulance corps headquarters, a renovated town court, and improved school and police services.

Key actions

  • Tim Hubbard was sworn in as Town Supervisor, becoming the 64th supervisor of Riverhead.
  • Denise Merrifield was sworn in as Town Councilwoman.
  • Joanne Waski was sworn in as Town Councilwoman.
  • Lori Hulse was sworn in as Town Justice.
  • James Wooten was sworn in as Town Clerk.
  • Donna Brown was sworn in as Town Assessor, and the receiver of taxes was also noted as having been sworn in separately.

Discussed

  • The new supervisor announced plans to form a commission to explore redevelopment of EPCAL following cancellation of a prior sale, emphasizing smart and green growth.
  • The supervisor stated the town's master plan is nearing completion and will guide development for the next 10 to 20 years.
  • Plans were described to build a new headquarters for the town ambulance corps, whose current facility has been outgrown.
  • The old town hall is to be retrofitted into a new town court complex to replace substandard current conditions.
  • The supervisor committed to reinstating quarterly civic roundtable meetings, holding biweekly department head meetings, and conducting more detailed work sessions.
