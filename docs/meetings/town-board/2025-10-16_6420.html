October 16, 2025 — Town Board

Qualified and Eligible Public Hearing
69 min  ·  CivicClerk page

Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board held a public hearing to assess whether the Jazz Loft, a Stony Brook-based nonprofit arts organization, is qualified and eligible to acquire and redevelop the historic Vail-Levitt Theater on Main Street. The board closed the hearing but is accepting written public comments through October 24, 2025.

Public hearings & comment

  • The hearing focused on the Jazz Loft's proposed acquisition and restoration of the Vail-Levitt Theater, built in 1881 and described as the oldest theater on Long Island.
  • The Jazz Loft's representatives stated the organization has a purchase agreement for the property at a price of $150,000 and reported current cash of approximately $256,000 and equity of approximately $850,000.
  • Representatives said ticket revenues at the Jazz Loft have exceeded $3 million over the past five years and that community donors and the Rotary Club have pledged additional support.
  • A two-phase restoration plan prepared by a historic preservation consultant was presented, with phase one addressing structural and weatherproofing issues including the roof, and phase two covering aesthetic improvements.
  • The Jazz Loft outlined a proposed timeline of a soft opening in January 2026 and completion of renovations by July 2026.
  • A representative of Stony Brook Southampton described an ongoing successful concert series partnership with the Jazz Loft as evidence of the organization's track record.
  • Several community members spoke in support during public comment, including the executive director of the Long Island Aquarium citing tourism benefits.
  • The board closed the hearing and announced written comments will be accepted through October 24, 2025.

Key actions

  • The board voted to close the public hearing while keeping the record open for written comment until October 24, 2025.
