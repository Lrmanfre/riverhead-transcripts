Summary AI

The Town Board held a brief special meeting to accept the retirement of the outgoing Police Chief and promote Edward Frost to the provisional title of Chief of Police.

Key actions

  • The board adopted a resolution accepting the retirement of the longtime Chief of Police, who served over 42 years with the department including 23 years as chief.
  • The board adopted a resolution promoting Edward Frost to the provisional title of Chief of Police, and he was sworn in at the meeting.
  • Chief Frost is scheduled to hold three community meet-and-greet events in early September at the Senior Citizen Center, Town Hall, and Riley Avenue School.
