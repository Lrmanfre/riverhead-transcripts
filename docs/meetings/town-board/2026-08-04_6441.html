August 4, 2026 — Town Board

Town Board Meeting
169 min  ·  CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The board approved site plan and special permit for the Peconic River Hotel project, exempted RVAC's proposed $14 million ambulance barn from formal site plan review, issued a positive declaration for a golf course cottage zoning amendment, and passed dozens of administrative and personnel resolutions unanimously.

Key actions

  • The board unanimously approved a special permit and site plan for the Peconic River Hotel at 117–217 East Main Street (Resolution 746), despite public objections over Suffolk County Planning Commission environmental justice requirements and parking.
  • The board unanimously determined that RVAC's proposed ambulance barn at 1121 Osborne Avenue is exempt from formal site plan review under the Monroe balancing test (Resolution 764).
  • A public hearing on capital improvements for the Riverhead Ambulance District was scheduled for September 1, 2026 (Resolution 753).
  • The board unanimously issued a positive declaration and accepted a draft scope for a GEIS covering proposed golf course zoning amendments that would create a new TDR receiving district for golf cottages at six golf courses (Resolutions 747 and 748).
  • A public hearing on stiffer penalties for commercial sale of animals was held and left open for 10 days of written comment; proposed fines range from $500–$1,500 for a first offense up to $3,000–$5,000 for a third or more offense.
  • The board authorized two separate professional services agreements with Sound Engineering PC for traffic signal design at Osborne/Harrison avenues and South Jamesport/Railroad avenues (Resolutions 756 and 757).
  • A settlement of a tax certiorari lawsuit brought by United Riverhead Terminal Inc. against the town's Board of Assessors was approved unanimously; the settlement amount was not disclosed at the meeting (Resolution 760).
  • Eleven special event permits were approved unanimously, covering events including the Polish Festival, Foodie Fest, Hallockville Museum Farm fairs, a Veterans Day 5K, a haunted house, and fireworks at Riverhead Raceway on August 8.
  • The board adopted a gender-based violence policy related to federal funding and established or expanded the Wildlife Management Advisory Committee (Resolutions 733 and 732).
  • The board ratified a budget adjustment to temporarily fund pay-down of the Town Square bond, and separately noted that Riverhead Town Square LLC exercised a contractual right to extend its site-ready period by six months (Resolutions 762 and 26-762).
