February 6, 2024 — Town Board

Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board held a lengthy February 6 meeting covering towing fee caps, apprenticeship requirement changes, cannabis zoning debate, numerous personnel and contract approvals, and a heated public exchange over a proposed agritourism hotel and campaign contributions.

Key actions

  • A public hearing was opened on amending towing regulations to require conspicuous signage, mandate tow-company notification to Riverhead PD, and cap towing charges at $375 (municipal rate); the hearing was kept open for written comments through February 16.
  • A public hearing was opened on raising the apprenticeship requirement trigger from $250,000 to $750,000 and the square-footage threshold from 1,000 to 250,000 sq ft, with a subcontractor exemption at $100,000 and various exclusions; hearing kept open through February 16.
  • The board adopted resolutions appointing a water treatment plant operator trainee, a public safety dispatcher, a financial advisor, and bond counsel, all unanimously.
  • The board adopted a resolution authorizing a licensing agreement with Adventure Group LLC d/b/a Cousins Paintball Inc., with porta-potties confirmed as restroom facilities.
  • The board awarded a bid for installation of appurtenances in the Seacrest Estates subdivision (Riverhead Water District), adopted unanimously.
  • The town attorney confirmed that no generic environmental impact statement is required for proposed TDR sending/receiving area zoning because the receiving area is already designated in the draft code.
  • The Canal House litigation was reported as settled; a stipulation of settlement including fines is to be brought before the board at the next meeting for approval.
  • Pre-submission meetings on Tuesdays at 9:30 AM will be reopened to the public, with agendas posted the prior Friday and meetings held in the town boardroom.

Money

  • The Town Clerk's January 2024 monthly report totaled $10,475.45.
  • Tax Receiver collections for January ranged from approximately $87 million to $92.7 million across three reporting dates.
  • Building Department collections for January 2024 totaled $114,459.
  • The Water District Superintendent testified that the lowest bid on a well drilling contract came in just under $500,000 while the second bid was just under $900,000, citing the apprenticeship requirement as undermining competitive bidding.

Public hearings & comment

  • A resident reported being charged $600 for a tow from a shopping plaza after parking for a bus service, and called for board action.
  • Multiple cannabis licensees and advocates urged the board to vote on cannabis zoning at the February 21 hearing, raising concerns about a 2,500-foot setback versus the state's 1,000-foot standard and a limited number of viable parcels; staff identified 144 eligible parcels with two or three applications pending.
