January 3, 2024 — Town Board

Town Board Meeting

Summary AI

The newly seated Riverhead Town Board held its first meeting of 2024, approving salaries, making numerous personnel appointments, and passing a six-month industrial moratorium for the Calverton area while also amending EPCAL zoning rules.

Key actions

  • The board set salaries for elected officials and employees across all town departments for 2024, approving numerous individual salary adjustments unanimously.
  • Town Attorney Eric Howard was reappointed; one board member noted a family relationship but voted yes after consulting the deputy town attorney on applicable ethics rules.
  • Edward Deniseski was appointed Planning Board chairperson and John Hogan as vice chair; Ken Zelnicki was appointed as a new Planning Board member, with one board member abstaining due to a distant family relationship.
  • Otto Whitmire was appointed Zoning Board of Appeals chairperson and Ralph Gazzillo was reappointed and named vice chair, following the accepted resignation of a long-serving ZBA member.
  • A six-month industrial moratorium for the Calverton area (Chapter 303) was adopted as amended, requiring a supermajority; one board member voted no and another voted yes citing the comprehensive plan's near completion.
  • The board amended Chapter 301 zoning to prohibit commercial, cargo, and freight airports at EPCAL while expressly permitting fueling, hangaring, and private plane operations ancillary to permitted uses; one member voted no, stating concern that the exception would allow ongoing flight activity residents oppose.
  • The board authorized a public notice for a local law amending waterway regulations, with the supervisor indicating it could reopen East Creek to recreational clamming for town residents with a permit.
  • A portable nine-unit restroom facility was approved for Veterans Memorial Park as an interim measure pending a permanent structure expected in spring.

Money

  • The tax receiver reported total tax collections of approximately $8.1 million as of December 21, 2023.
  • The town accepted a grant from FM Global consisting of a digital camera and lighting system donated to the fire marshals.
  • The town is processing a $5.5 million EPA grant and separate state funding under the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law to address contaminated water in Manorville; a multi-agency coordination meeting was scheduled for January 11.

Public hearings & comment

  • Multiple residents from Wading River and Calverton urged passage of the industrial moratorium and expressed concern that a project referred to as HK Ventures may be exempt from it.
  • A resident raised questions about whether a proposed Riverhead Charter School expansion onto approximately 71 acres along Sound Avenue is permitted in an APZ agricultural zoning district, citing code language that allows only private educational institutions as a special permit use.
  • A Manorville resident reported that MTBE levels in her well water recently exceeded allowable limits and that acetone has also been detected, calling for faster action on the water remediation effort.
