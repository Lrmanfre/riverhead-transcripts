September 15, 2026 — Town Board

Town Board Meeting

Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board held three public hearings on code amendments (fireworks permitting, special events, and procurement policy), left the comment period open until September 25th, and adopted most items on a lengthy resolution agenda while tabling the school resource officer contract renewal amid concerns about incomplete terms.

Public hearings & comment

  • The board held hearings on amending Chapter 231 (fireworks/pyrotechnics permitting, moving review to the Fire Marshal's office), Chapter 255 (special events, requiring fireworks permits per Chapter 231), and Chapter 115 (procurement policy, adding federal grant compliance standards); all three remain open for written comment until September 25th.
  • Several residents spoke in support of Resolution 843/854 funding the restoration of the Wading River Duck Ponds, including mention of a Suffolk County downtown revitalization grant and roughly $3.5 million in additional grant funding being pursued with Congressman LaLota's office.
  • Multiple residents raised concerns about an alleged ICE enforcement incident on Labor Day near Marcy Avenue in Riverhead and asked whether the town board or police department was aware or had responded.
  • A speaker alleged that letters he and another resident submitted were improperly withheld from the agenda packet by the Town Clerk, calling it a First Amendment concern.
  • Public comments also addressed a dispute involving a Town Board member's social media post about a resident, with calls for apology and reconciliation, and a proposal to revive a 1990s minority entrepreneurship training program.

Key actions

  • The board adopted the Wading River Duck Ponds capital project budget (Resolution 843) and awarded an engineering assessment contract to M&J Engineering, D.P.C. for the project (Resolution 854).
  • The board approved a sewer district extension to serve 840 Old Country Road (Suffolk County Tax Map #0600-82-03-9.1) under Resolution 855.
  • The board approved a budget adjustment for HVAC replacement at the George Young Community Center (Resolution 844) and awarded the related HVAC replacement bid (Resolution 869).
  • The board approved an Ambulance District budget adjustment for repairs to the Armory Building, allowing RVAC to operate from the Armory during construction of a new ambulance facility (Resolution 842).
  • The board tabled ratification of the renewal and amendment agreement for the Riverhead Central School District School Resource Officer Program (Resolution 856) due to concerns about an incomplete contract, incorrect dates, and overtime provisions.
  • The board accepted retirements of two police officers, a police detective, and an account clerk, and ratified the resignation of a Fire Marshal I.

Money

  • The Receiver of Taxes reported August 2026 monthly utility payments of $831,791.95.
  • The Town Clerk reported August 2026 monthly collections of $12,911.25.
  • The board approved a budget adjustment for 2026 records management and digital conversion (Resolution 877).
