March 3, 2026 — Town Board

Town Board Meeting
60 min  ·  CivicClerk page

Summary AI

The Town Board approved roughly two dozen resolutions covering personnel, special events, water and fire-code amendments, and a federal funding application. A public hearing on a new no-parking rule on Northern Parkway drew resident concerns about a nearby medical office, and public comment centered on ICE enforcement activity near schools and the town hockey rink's certificate of occupancy.

Key actions

  • The board adopted a resolution authorizing staff to apply for federal congressionally directed spending for Riverhead Water District extensions 96 and 97 and police department equipment, amending the grant amount to approximately $4,092,000.
  • The board approved three special-event permits for Jamesport Farm Brewery events (a food truck rodeo, a car show, and a Fresh Hop Festival) and separate permits for an East End Arts community mosaic event and a Rise Life Services car show.
  • The board authorized public notices to consider amendments to town code chapters governing use-tenancy fire marshal review and fire safety change-of-tenancy review.
  • A water district capital project budget was adopted and a highway department budget transfer for additional road salt was approved.
  • Four people were appointed or reappointed to the Town Agricultural Advisory Committee.
  • Two staff reclassifications, a new Recreation Department account clerk, and a new building plans examiner were approved.

Public hearings & comment

  • A public hearing was held on a proposed amendment to prohibit parking on the north side of Northern Parkway from Roanoke Avenue eastward for 500 feet, weekdays 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.; the hearing was closed with a 10-day written-comment period ending March 13.
  • Residents on Northern Parkway testified that the parking restriction would not solve underlying problems and raised concerns about fire-lane clearance, ADA accessibility, and certificate-of-occupancy compliance at an adjacent medical office.
  • Several residents and an advocacy organization representative asked the board to consider a public-safety resolution related to federal immigration enforcement activity near schools and in residential neighborhoods.
  • A caller raised concerns about the town hockey rink's outstanding final certificate of occupancy and an unresolved Freedom of Information Act request about rink repairs; the supervisor noted an outside engineering review had been completed and the rink is operating.

Discussed

  • The supervisor addressed the hockey rink dome incident, stating it occurred during a power outage rather than the blizzard, an outside engineer cleared the facility, and the fire marshal was involved.
  • A board member disclosed purchase of a 108-acre equestrian farm in Jamesport with development rights already sold to a farmland preservation program; a caller suggested the board seek a formal conflict-of-interest opinion regarding future agritourism or transfer-of-development-rights matters.
  • The supervisor warned residents about phishing scams impersonating town departments and noted the building department does not accept electronic payment.

Affects residents

  • Residents on and near Northern Parkway should monitor the proposed no-parking rule and may submit written comments through March 13.
