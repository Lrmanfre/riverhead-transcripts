Summary AI

The Riverhead Town Board held four public hearings, passed roughly three dozen resolutions, and addressed a flood watch, storm preparations, and a police rescue during a house fire.

Key actions

  • The board approved a change order for Town Hall renovation work at 4 West 2nd Street.
  • A capital project for a Downtown Parkway was established.
  • The board accepted the Final Generic Environmental Impact Statement (FGEIS) for the comprehensive plan update, with the document to be posted online for public review afterward.
  • A site plan application for a property on Scott Avenue/Calvin Street received preliminary approval with conditions.
  • The board authorized a license agreement allowing the police department to conduct DWI enforcement operations at EPCAL through year-end.
  • A public hearing was scheduled for a special permit application by Riverhead Charter School for portable trailers at 5117 Sound Avenue.
  • The residential solid waste collection and disposal service rebid was ratified, and the bid for police uniforms was awarded.
  • Andrew Smith was promoted to Chief Fire Marshal.

Money

  • Building Department collections for July totaled $211,440.
  • Community benefit funds from NextEra were approved for expenditure toward agricultural, open space, and community purposes.
  • Budget transfers were approved for a Veterans Memorial Park parking lot, a code enforcement vehicle, and water and sewer district capital projects.

Public hearings & comment

  • A public hearing on a special permit to convert an office building at 431 Griffin Avenue back to a single-family dwelling was closed; written comments accepted through August 16.
  • A neighboring property owner's attorney asked the board to require the applicant to record a covenant limiting the property to single-family use.
  • Public hearings on zoning code clarifications for one-family dwellings, a noise ordinance amendment allowing overnight parking-lot paving by application, and front-yard parking restriction enforcement changes were all closed with written comment periods through August 16.
