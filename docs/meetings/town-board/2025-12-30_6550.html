December 30, 2025 — Town Board

Special Town Board Meeting
25 min  ·  CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Town Board held a special meeting on December 30, 2025, passing two resolutions: one to fund a replacement dump truck for the Highway Department and one to transfer $75,000 in Community Development Block Grant funds to the Jazz Loft for repairs to the Vale-Levitt building.

Key actions

  • The board approved a budget adjustment to purchase a new dump truck for the Highway Department, replacing a truck destroyed by fire during a snowstorm on Sound Avenue.
  • The board approved a transfer of $75,000 in Suffolk County Community Development Block Grant funds to the Jazz Loft, a nonprofit, to repair and restore the historic Vale-Levitt building; one board member voted no.
  • One board member stated the CDBG money originates from county funds and expressed concern that the Jazz Loft had not disclosed a funding gap at its qualifying hearing.

Discussed

  • The Highway Superintendent noted the recent snowstorm was challenging due to staff illness, equipment issues, and the truck fire, and thanked employees from other town departments who assisted.
  • A resident raised concerns about an immigration enforcement incident near the State Police headquarters and asked for a public update on pending litigation involving an entity referred to as CAT; the board said a motion to dismiss is pending.
  • A resident suggested the recently demolished downtown site could be reconsidered as open green space rather than a hotel development, citing Greenport as a comparable example.
  • Outgoing Supervisor acknowledged ten years in public office and introduced the incoming supervisor, who was present in the audience.
