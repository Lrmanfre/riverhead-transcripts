July 24, 2025 — Town Board

Special Town Board Meeting
4 min  ·  CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Town Board unanimously approved a resolution authorizing a state Consolidated Funding Application under the Pro Housing Program to pursue pass-through grant funding for infrastructure at a proposed mixed-use development at 426 West Main Street.

Key actions

  • The board passed Resolution 2025-643, authorizing a CFA 2025 Pro Housing Program funding application to New York State.
  • If awarded, the grant would pass through the town to a developer for infrastructure costs—including sewer, water, electrical, and traffic work—at a planned market-rate apartment building with retail and restaurant space at 426 West Main Street.
