November 5, 2026 — Town Board

Town Board Meeting

Summary AI

The Town Board held three public hearings (a water main extension, a veterans tax exemption, and the 2027 budget), all kept open for written comment until mid-November, and tabled a resolution to hire outside counsel for the Peconic Bay region's Community Preservation Fund committee after board and public concerns about cost and process.

Public hearings & comment

  • A public hearing on a lateral water main extension at 1001 Scott Avenue, Calverton (a developer-funded $347,000 project to relocate a 12-inch water main) was held open for written comment until November 16.
  • A public hearing on a local law creating a full property tax exemption for permanently and totally disabled veterans drew support from veterans and American Legion members; the town assessor flagged wording errors to be corrected, and the hearing was left open for written comment until November 16.
  • A public hearing on the proposed 2027 budget noted the tax levy increase is set at 1.79%, below the state tax cap of 2.79%; no public comments were made and it was held open until November 16.
  • During public comment, a resident raised questions about a request-for-proposals process for a Science Center project and the earlier no-bid approach used for a hotel project, and suggested the town consider requiring a security deposit for film/event use of Reeves Park Beach.

Key actions

  • The board tabled Resolution 2026-980, which would have authorized hiring attorney Mary Wilson for the Peconic Bay region's Community Preservation Fund (CPF) advisory committee, after board members and residents questioned the mandatory nature of the state-required arrangement, the attorney's Florida residence, travel billing, and lack of invoices to date.
  • The board amended Resolution 2026-975 appointing an automotive equipment operator to the Highway Department to correct a wording error before adopting it.
  • The board adopted resolutions covering budget transfers for the Water District and general liability insurance claims, personnel appointments and a resignation (including an Assistant Chief Fire Marshal), a Chapter 229 materials-importation application for a self-storage facility at 61 Main Road, acceptance of a letter of credit from Red's Adventures LLC (Summerwind Farm), approval of appraisals for the Young's Farm Association development-rights acquisition, agreements with Casa Projects Group LLC and the Honorable Lori Hulse, landfill monitoring contract extensions, notices to consider local law amendments to the Water and Traffic/Parking chapters, a scheduled public hearing on zoning district amendments, bid notices for water district well/pump testing and a sewer district SCADA system, extensions of auto parts and construction debris disposal bids, a special event approval for the BIDMA Merry Market, payment of bills, and a declaration of public emergency for yard waste removal at the Young's Avenue facility.
