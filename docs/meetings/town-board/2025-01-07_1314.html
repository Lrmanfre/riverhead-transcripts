January 7, 2025 — Town Board

Town Board Meeting

Summary AI

The board held a public hearing on the Sandy Pond Golf Course special permit application and passed roughly 88 resolutions setting 2025 salaries, making appointments, and handling administrative matters, including a contested 4-1 vote to ratify a $50,000 settlement with Island Water Park.

Key actions

  • The board voted unanimously to set 2025 salaries for elected officials, general town employees, police, highway, water district, and other personnel across more than a dozen resolutions.
  • Several employees were reclassified to higher job titles, including multiple heavy equipment operators promoted to construction equipment operator.
  • The board ratified a $50,000 civil penalty settlement of a legal action against Island Water Park, with one member voting no citing the operator's history of repeat code violations and calling the penalty insufficient.
  • After the settlement vote, the board untabled and approved scheduling a public hearing on Island Water Park's pending site plan amendment application.
  • A resolution awarding a rebid for the Meeting House Creek stormwater wetland and habitat restoration project was recalled and then defeated unanimously after staff clarified the town intends to issue a new rebid instead.
  • The board authorized the Community Development Department to apply for the New York State ADU Plus One Program.
  • A license agreement with the Research Foundation of SUNY was approved for installation of a fish passage camera at a town park.
  • The board adopted a local law amending town code to set term limits for town justices.

Public hearings & comment

  • A public hearing was held on a special permit application from Sandy Pond Links at 1495 Roanoke Avenue for a roughly 3,100-square-foot clubhouse, a property manager's residence, three trade shop buildings, and 67 additional parking spaces; the hearing was closed and kept open for written comment until January 17, 2025.
  • One commenter argued the project's environmental review was improperly segmented by reviewing the mini-golf phase and the clubhouse/trade shop phase separately; the senior planner and the applicant's attorney disputed that characterization and addressed the timeline for the non-conforming use question.
  • Multiple residents spoke against ratifying the Island Water Park settlement, citing unpermitted construction, DEC violations related to the go-kart track and expanded parking lot, and the adequacy of the $50,000 penalty.
  • A resident raised questions about PFAS contamination risks related to an expansion of the West Wastewater Treatment Plant and asked the board to ensure biosolids are tested before any land application.

Affects residents

  • The town is accepting applications for part-time police officers; candidates must be at least six months short of 21 and no older than 35.
  • Residents may submit written comments on the Sandy Pond Golf Course special permit application through January 17, 2025.
