Town Board Meeting
CivicClerk page

Summary AI

The Town Board held a brief special session, adopting three resolutions covering an environmental review and public hearing notice for a National Grid facility upgrade and a home rule request supporting retroactive retirement system membership for a named individual.

Key actions

  • The board approved a negative declaration under SEQRA for a National Grid facility upgrade at 8 Mill Road and authorized a public hearing notice for the associated special permit application.
  • The board adopted a home rule resolution requesting state legislative support for a bill that would grant retroactive Tier 2 membership in the New York State and local police and fire retirement system to a named town employee.
  • The board entered executive session to discuss potential litigation, real property acquisition, a personnel matter, and a professional services contract.
