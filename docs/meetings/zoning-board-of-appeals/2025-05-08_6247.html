May 8, 2025 — Zoning Board of Appeals

Zoning Board Meeting

Summary AI

The Riverhead ZBA granted variances for three residential properties and closed the public comment period on a cannabis dispensary application, with a decision reserved for June 12, 2025.

Key actions

  • Appeal 2025-006 (George Elia, 71 Pine Avenue, Aquebogue): The board re-opened and granted rear yard setback and related variances, conditioned on the roofed overhang remaining an unenclosed porch, removal of a small platform along the property line, and all prior conditions from the March 2025 determination remaining in effect.
  • Appeal 2025-015 (Deborah Thompson, 6 Gladys Road, Wading River): Variances for a proposed single-family dwelling were granted, including front yard, rear yard, and side yard abutting street setbacks, with a condition that the rear yard vegetative buffer required by the 2022 ZBA determination be maintained.
  • Appeal 2025-017 (Lawrence Luce, 752 Soundshore Road, Jamesport): Variances to legalize an existing side-yard deck near a bluff were granted, conditioned on no further encroachment into the coastal erosion hazard area and no foundation added beyond the existing footings.
  • The cannabis retail dispensary application for 1086 Old Country Road (Appeal seeking relief from the 1,000-foot school proximity rule) had its written comment period closed; a decision is reserved for the June 12, 2025 ZBA meeting.
  • The May 22, 2025 ZBA meeting is canceled due to no pending appeals; the next meeting is June 12, 2025.
