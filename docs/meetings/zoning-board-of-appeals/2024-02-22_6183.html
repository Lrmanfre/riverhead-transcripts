Summary AI

The Zoning Board of Appeals met on February 22, 2024, and granted variances in all four cases heard.

Key actions

  • A variance was granted for an accessory structure in the front yard at 118 Northside Road, Wading River (Appeal 2023-052).
  • Variances for a directory sign at 1352 East Main Street were granted, with conditions requiring removal of the existing marquee and all unpermitted tenant signs (Appeal 2023-057).
  • A rear-yard setback variance was granted for an in-ground pool installed at 16 feet instead of the required 20 feet at 42 Julia's Way, Wading River (Appeal 2024-002).
  • A front-yard setback variance was granted to allow elevation of a residence and addition of a new porch at 54 Beach Road, Laurel (Appeal 2024-005).
