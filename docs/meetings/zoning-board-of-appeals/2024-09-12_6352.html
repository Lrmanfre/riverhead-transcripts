September 12, 2024 — Zoning Board of Appeals

Zoning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page

Summary AI

The Riverhead Zoning Board of Appeals met on September 12, 2024, approving variances or extensions for five properties and adjourning minutes from the prior meeting.

Key actions

  • An extension request for Appeal 2021-004 at 40 Manor Lane, Jamesport (minor subdivision) was granted, setting a final expiration date of February 25, 2025, with no further extensions allowed.
  • Variances for a proposed two-lot minor subdivision at 934 Soundshore Road, Jamesport (Susan McCarthy) were approved, with conditions including removal of a shed on the vacant parcel, combined minimum side yards of 25 feet with no less than 10 feet per side, and required Planning Board and Suffolk County Health Department approval.
  • Variances for a proposed two-lot minor subdivision at 230 West Main Street, Riverhead (Riverhead Property LLC) were approved, allowing each lot a front width of 45.11 feet where 50 feet is required; the board recommended the Planning Board consider a shared driveway and right-turn-only exit for traffic safety.
  • A variance to legalize a relocated accessory shed at 189 Ackerley Street, Riverhead (Gabrielle Sharbot Watts) was approved, requiring submission of a final survey confirming the shed's new location.
  • Variances for a proposed covered wraparound porch at 53 South Jamesport Avenue, Jamesport (James Goodman) were approved, permitting a side-yard-abutting-street setback of 15.84 feet and impervious surface coverage of approximately 30.61 percent.
  • Variances for a proposed detached garage at 61 Arrowhead Avenue, Riverhead (Thomas Daly) were approved, allowing a 6-foot accessory side yard setback and a 7.85-foot setback to the dwelling.
  • Minutes from the August 8, 2024 meeting were approved, and the board adjourned to September 26, 2024.
