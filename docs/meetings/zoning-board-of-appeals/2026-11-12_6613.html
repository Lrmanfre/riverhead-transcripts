November 12, 2026 — Zoning Board of Appeals

Summary AI

The Zoning Board of Appeals granted extensions and variances for several Riverhead properties, approved a gym project on Route 25A in Wading River with modified driveway access conditions, and deemed two applications abandoned due to lack of applicant response.

Key actions

  • The board granted a one-year extension (to November 13, 2027) for an appeal at 7 Warner Court, Calverton.
  • An adjournment to February 25 was granted for the Lincoln Street Opportunity Zone Fund LLC appeal.
  • A final adjournment with a January 14, 2027 hearing date was set for an appeal at 178 Main Road, Riverhead (Maria Salguero), with required survey and denial-letter documents due by December 14, 2026 or the application will be deemed abandoned.
  • The board approved variances for a proposed two-story gym building at Route 25A, Wading River (Three Diamonds Property), including front yard depth, landscape buffer, and driveway access relief, with the applicant agreeing to make the Debbie Lane access ingress-only and keep North Country Road access as ingress/egress, plus conditions on landscape buffering around the outdoor exercise area.
  • The board granted a front yard setback variance for a covered porch addition at 40 Nautical Drive, Riverhead.
  • A decision on reopening special exceptions for an antique/classic car restoration business at 1500 West Main Street (Phase One Auto Body) was reserved for December 10, pending conditions requiring planning and building staff approval of screened outdoor storage.
  • An appeal for a property at 413 Craderville Avenue was formally deemed abandoned due to the applicant's failure to provide required documentation after multiple adjournments, with no refund of fees.
  • Two previously reserved decisions (Sweezy Avenue and Route 25A, Wading River appeals) were postponed to December 10 due to town computer problems.

Public hearings & comment

  • At the Three Diamonds Property hearing, board members questioned whether the building size and outdoor turf/training area could be reduced to lessen the need for setback variances; the applicant and engineer argued the lot's unique triangular, three-front-yard configuration limited design options.
  • The highway superintendent recommended the Debbie Lane access be ingress-only and the North Country Road access be egress-only, while the fire marshal had supported two full ingress/egress points; the applicant proposed a compromise of ingress-only on Debbie Lane and full ingress/egress on North Country Road, which the board accepted.
  • At the Phase One Auto Body hearing, the property owner and operator described current and proposed restoration operations, outdoor storage needs for vehicle parts and equipment, noise/dust mitigation measures, and site buffering from neighboring properties.
