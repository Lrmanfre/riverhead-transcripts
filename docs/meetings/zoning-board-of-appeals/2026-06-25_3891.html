June 25, 2026 — Zoning Board of Appeals

CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Zoning Board of Appeals granted sign variances to RC Adventures LLC for a multi-tenant commercial development at 365 Mill Road, Riverhead. One board member abstained due to a financial interest in the property owner.

Key actions

  • The board approved variances allowing three monument directory signs instead of the one permitted by code, one sign at 15 feet from the Old Country Road sideline instead of the required 25 feet, and tenant panels of 10 square feet instead of the 8-square-foot maximum.
  • Approval was granted subject to final Architectural Review Board sign-off on the design.
  • One board member abstained citing ownership of stock in the Long Island Cauliflower Association, which owns the property.
