February 13, 2025 — Zoning Board of Appeals

Zoning Board Meeting

Summary AI

The Zoning Board of Appeals denied a use variance for a proposed retail cannabis dispensary, approved a coastal erosion area variance for a Jamesport residential pool, accepted a withdrawal of one appeal, and reserved decision on a disputed pool-house addition in Riverhead.

Key actions

  • Appeal 2024-035 (Elizabeth McGrath, 1201 Ostrander Avenue): the board denied the use variance request for a proposed retail cannabis dispensary, finding the applicant failed to demonstrate unnecessary hardship or meet the required elements for a use variance.
  • Appeal 2024-031 (Jason and Jamie Pastrano, 834 Soundshore Road, Jamesport): the board granted relief under the Coastal Erosion Hazard Area regulations for a single-family dwelling and in-ground pool based on amended plans dated January 29, 2025, which relocated improvements landward of the 50-foot CEHA setback and removed the proposed hot tub.
  • Appeal 2024-036 (Portia Brown Trent, 650 Elton Street): the application was withdrawn before the public hearing was held.
  • Appeal 2023-033 (Kathleen Glass, 120 Louise Court): the board reserved its decision, to be issued at the next meeting on February 27, 2025, after hearing testimony about a revised pool-house and cabana proposal and neighbor objections regarding stormwater runoff and structure size.

Public hearings & comment

  • A neighbor to the west of the Glass property testified at length against the pool-house proposal, citing ongoing stormwater runoff and sand accumulation on their driveway and concerns about the size of the enclosed and roofed areas.
  • The applicant's representative presented a comparative analysis showing the currently proposed roofed area of 798 square feet and enclosed area of 323 square feet, down significantly from a prior proposal, and submitted an engineering drainage report and topographical survey.
  • Correspondence from two neighboring properties objecting to the Glass application was entered into the record before the hearing.

Discussed

  • Board members questioned whether stormwater runoff onto the neighbor's property originated from a soil mound on the Glass lots or from an adjacent flag lot entrance, with conflicting accounts from both sides.
  • The board noted that stormwater and construction-hours complaints are enforcement matters for code enforcement and the highway department, separate from the variance questions before the ZBA.
