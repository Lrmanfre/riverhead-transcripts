November 14, 2024 — Zoning Board of Appeals

Summary AI

The Riverhead Zoning Board of Appeals held three variance hearings on November 14, 2024, approving two applications and adjourning a third pending revised plans.

Key actions

  • A side-yard setback variance for two sheds on a Calverton property was approved, conditioned on the owner obtaining all outstanding certificates of occupancy including one for an existing in-ground pool.
  • Setback variances for front-yard, rear-yard, and side-yard-abutting-road relief were granted to owners of a property on Gregory Way in Riverhead to allow additions including an expanded living room and a garage with unfinished upper-floor space.
  • A coastal hazard area variance application for a property at 834 Soundshore Road in Jamesport — seeking to demolish an existing bungalow and build a new two-story house, in-ground pool, and accessory structure — was adjourned to the December 12, 2024 meeting so the applicant can submit a substantially amended site plan.

Public hearings & comment

  • Board counsel recommended the Soundshore Road applicant amend and correct multiple elements of the application, including fixing a bedroom-count discrepancy, showing bluff setback distances on the survey, addressing excavation and fill-removal plans, resolving the status of a potentially unpermitted wetland on the property, and seeking to formally amend a 2010 ZBA variance that restricted the existing accessory structure from habitable use.
  • A board member raised concern about the proposed pool being sited near the bluff line and suggested relocating it landward of the house toward the street side of the property.
