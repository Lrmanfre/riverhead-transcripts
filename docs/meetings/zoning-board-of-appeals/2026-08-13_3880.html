August 13, 2026 — Zoning Board of Appeals

Summary AI

The Zoning Board of Appeals met August 13, 2026, approved a one-year extension for one prior appeal, and granted variances for as-built structures at a Jamesport property with several conditions.

Key actions

  • A one-year extension was approved for Appeal 2025-029 (25 Nautical Drive, Riverhead), pushing the expiration to September 11, 2027.
  • Appeal 2026-012 for 735 Manor Lane, Jamesport was granted variances to legalize a pergola in the front yard and three sheds, including one that was on the property line.
  • Conditions of approval require the shed on the property line to be moved to at least five feet from the line, any structures or goat shelters inside the 25-foot agricultural buffer to be removed, and the pergola chimney and pool fence to be reviewed and approved by the appropriate building department official.
