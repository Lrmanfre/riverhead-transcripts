Summary AI

The Zoning Board of Appeals met on July 24, 2025 and acted on four matters: a permit extension and three variance requests, all of which were granted. The next meeting is August 28, 2025; there is no meeting on August 14.

Key actions

  • A one-year extension was granted for appeal 2024-020 (Frank Stepnoski, 30 Dolphin Way, Riverhead) for a proposed garage and existing shed.
  • An amendment to appeal 2023-049 was granted for a Habitat-affiliated property on behalf of a future homeowner, recognizing as-built deviations including 26% impervious coverage and slightly reduced setbacks caused by a foundation poured six inches off.
  • Variances for side yard, rear yard, and impervious surface were granted for Leonard and Denise Richardson (20 Oak Drive, Jamesport) for an open-sided pergola over an above-ground therapeutic pool, on condition that all sides remain open.
  • Multiple setback and impervious coverage variances were granted for Carol Schick (4 Park Drive, Riverhead) to demolish an existing cottage and construct a two-story dwelling on a preexisting undersized lot in the Reeds Park community.
