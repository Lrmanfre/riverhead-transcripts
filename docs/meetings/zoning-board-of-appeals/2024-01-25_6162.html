January 25, 2024 — Zoning Board of Appeals

Zoning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page
Agenda PDF

Summary AI

The Zoning Board of Appeals held its first meeting of 2024, approving a settlement agreement with Gershow Recycling and granting multiple variances for a two-lot unmerger on Park Road.

Key actions

  • The board unanimously passed Resolution 2024-01, authorizing the chair to execute a settlement agreement and mutual release with Gershow Recycling of Riverhead.
  • The board unanimously granted variances for Amanda Furmentier at 86 Park Road (Tax Map 600-16-3-3.1) for a two-lot unmerger, including lot area, lot width, impervious surface coverage, a shed rear setback, and conversion of a seasonal accessory apartment to a principal single-family dwelling on Lot 2, subject to conditions including obtaining a certificate of occupancy, removing highway right-of-way encroachments, and getting highway apron approval.

Discussed

  • The next meeting is scheduled for February 8, 2024.
