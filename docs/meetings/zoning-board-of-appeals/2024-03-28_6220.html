March 28, 2024 — Zoning Board of Appeals

Zoning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page

Summary AI

The Zoning Board of Appeals granted two variance applications at its March 28, 2024 meeting: one for a new house on Birch Lane in Wading River and one for a ground-mounted solar array on Peconic Bay Boulevard in Laurel.

Key actions

  • The board granted variances for an application by Dorothy DeFranco for a residence on Birch Lane, Wading River, approving a front yard setback of 30 feet (60 feet required), a combined side yard setback of 49.3 feet (65 feet required), a rear yard setback of 36 feet (75 feet required), and impervious surface coverage of 34.5% (15% maximum allowed), with conditions that privacy bushes be planted around the perimeter and an existing concrete slab shown on the survey be removed.
  • The board granted a variance for Frank McVeigh at 1589 Peconic Bay Boulevard, Laurel, to install a ground-mounted solar array in the front yard, where accessory solar structures in the front yard are not permitted, subject to the condition that a vegetative buffer along the westerly side of the property be maintained to shield the adjacent property from view.
  • The board approved the minutes of its previous meeting and scheduled its next meeting for April 11, 2024.

Public hearings & comment

  • Neighbors on Birch Lane testified about concerns regarding the proposed house's proximity to their property lines and driveway access; both indicated their questions were answered after reviewing the survey.
  • No members of the public commented on the Peconic Bay Boulevard solar application.
