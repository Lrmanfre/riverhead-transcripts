September 26, 2024 — Zoning Board of Appeals

Zoning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page

Summary AI

The Riverhead Zoning Board of Appeals held its September 26 meeting, granting variances in two cases, adjourning one case to October 10 for a permit amendment, and approving meeting minutes.

Key actions

  • Variances for Kelly Development Corp at 32 Pine Avenue, Aquebogue were granted for a side yard setback of 5 feet (25 feet required) and a combined side yard of 19.5 feet (55 feet required), subject to New York State DEC approval, town CAC approval, Suffolk County Health Department approval, and FEMA flood compliance.
  • Variances for the property at 55 Jacobs Place, Aquebogue were granted for an addition, front deck, above-ground pool, and existing shed, including relief from front-yard accessory structure restrictions, a 7.4-foot side setback (25 feet required), a 16.4-foot rear setback (60 feet required), and 39% impervious surface coverage (15% permitted maximum).
  • Appeal 2024-024 for 159 Sunset Boulevard, Wading River — a proposed 12-by-24-foot accessory storage structure — was adjourned to October 10, 2024, so the applicant can amend the permit application to legalize an existing interior conversion of a separate accessory structure to habitable space.
  • Minutes from the September 12, 2024 meeting were approved.
  • The next meeting is scheduled for October 10, 2024.
