June 27, 2024 — Zoning Board of Appeals

Zoning Board Meeting
CivicClerk page

Summary AI

The Zoning Board of Appeals denied a parking variance for a downtown townhouse project and adjourned a front-yard shed legalization request pending an amended application.

Key actions

  • The board unanimously denied the parking stall variance for Second Street Townhouses at 46 East Second Street, finding the shortfall self-created and determining that adding vehicles would worsen traffic and parking conditions on the narrow road.
  • The board adjourned appeal number 2024-0117, a request to legalize an accessory structure in a front yard at 189 Ackerley Street, and directed the applicant to meet with a building inspector to identify a compliant rear- or side-yard location before submitting an amended application.
  • The board accepted the meeting minutes.

Public hearings & comment

  • For the Second Street Townhouses application, the board considered a Planning Board resolution and a highway superintendent inspection report regarding traffic and parking on East Second and Third Streets, both entered into the record.
  • The applicant for the Second Street project presented two alternative site plan options to reach the required 12 parking stalls, but board counsel advised that approving a site plan deviation is outside the ZBA's authority and that one option would remove required accessible parking, which is not permissible.
  • The applicant for the Ackerley Street shed acknowledged no other homes in the neighborhood have an accessory structure in a front yard; board members noted the front-yard location could set an undesirable precedent and encouraged exploring rear-yard alternatives.

Affects residents

  • The denial of the Second Street Townhouses parking variance means the project cannot proceed in its current form without resolving the one-space parking shortfall.
  • The Ackerley Street shed case remains open; the applicant must submit an amended application before the matter can be decided, with the next meeting scheduled for July 11.
