February 27, 2025 — Zoning Board of Appeals

Zoning Board Meeting

Summary AI

The Riverhead Zoning Board of Appeals held its February 27 meeting, granting variances in three cases and closing a fourth with a reserved decision pending environmental review.

Key actions

  • Appeal 2020-033 (Kathleen Glass, 120 Louise Court, Riverhead): The board approved an amended variance for a front-yard pool pavilion, trellis, and enclosed meditation space with bathroom, subject to conditions limiting the structure to seasonal use, prohibiting heating, year-round water supply, kitchen or laundry facilities beyond a sink and refrigerator, overnight accommodations, and any business use.
  • Appeal 2024-038 (Claude Maselli, 99 Cliff Road East, Wading River): The board granted a variance allowing as-built impervious surface coverage of 43.4% where the maximum permitted in the RB-80 zone is 15%, based on the undersized preexisting nonconforming lot.
  • Appeal 2025-004 (Herb Steiger, 131-135 Hidden Acres Path, Wading River): The board granted a variance for a proposed in-ground pool with a side-yard abutting-street setback of 18 feet where 60 feet is required, conditioned on relocating an existing shed to at least 5 feet from all side and rear property lines as required by town code.
  • Appeal 2024-034 (Fisher Realty Group, 940–946 West Main Street, Riverhead): The board closed the public hearing with a reserved decision to be issued at a future date after the Planning Board, acting as lead agency, completes its SEQRA review; the applicant noted revised calculations reduced the FAR variance request to 75.17% and the impervious coverage figure to approximately 49.95%, which the applicant contends eliminates the landscaping variance.
  • Minutes from the January 23, 2025 meeting were approved, and the next meeting was set for March 13, 2025.
