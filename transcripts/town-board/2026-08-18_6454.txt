The impetus for the proposed code amendment was initiated when an application for a use permit was submitted to the building department at the end of April for a property located on Roanoke Avenue where an applicant sought to establish a hair treatment studio which is akin to a hair salon. The use is not currently listed as a permitted use within the business PB zoning use district which currently allows for a range of professional offices as a permitted use. The code currently lists 19 different types of professional offices. Our current definition of professional office, which is located in Town Code Section 301-3, lists those professional offices as well as, quote, any other professional office determined by resolution of the Town Board to have similar impacts to those listed above, end quote. As the Board will recall, back on April 21st of this year, by Resolution No. 2026-403, the Board adopted a resolution finding that an esthetician's office, while not specifically enumerated in the list of professional offices had similar impacts to those that were specifically listed thus allowing an esthetician's office to be located at a property on Roanoke Avenue when the matter of the hair studio came across my desk it caused me to more closely examine those permitted uses and think of them in terms of impacts as that's what the code specifically directs the board to consider thus a code amendment was generated to allow personal services as a permitted use within the business PB zoning use district. Town Code currently defines personal services as quote a commercial use that provides specialized hands-on services directly to individuals as for personal or physical benefit. These services generally involve customer interaction and may include activities that focus on personal care, fitness, wellness, or leisure. Personal service establishments do not involve the provision of professional or technical services requiring specialized education, certification or licensure as seen in professional offices. Services provided are typically of a non-medical, non-therapeutic nature and do not involve significant office-based administrative or professional consultation functions. Personal services specifically includes fitness studios, beauty services, massage therapy, personal coaching, esthetician and skin care services, fortune telling or psychic readings, cultural or educational studios, pet grooming services, dry cleaning or laundry services, as well as any other use that the zoning officer determines to be similar in nature to those listed above. The nature of personal services are such that they are interior uses. They are generally not high traffic or trip generators and would not typically be anticipated to involve any outdoor activities or storage, which would create a detriment to the neighboring residential properties. The matter was initially discussed by the Town Board at a work session on June 9th of this year. Subsequently, went back to Code Revision Committee for further refinement, which included the proposal to make personal services a specially permitted use within the business PB zoning use district by special permit of the Town Board, with the exception of dry cleaning and laundry services, as well as the inclusion of limits on the hours of operation, which are proposed to be limited from 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. so in summary the proposed amendments to Town Code chapter 301 article 16 would by special permit of the town board permit personal services with the exception of dry cleaning and laundry services and would establish limits on the hours of operation of business uses within the business PB zoning use district from 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. it is worth noting that there are already several properties along the Roanoke Avenue corridor that have already been converted from single-family residential to some type of medical office or professional office any other property that would like to come in if they would like to convert to a commercial use or a personal service would need to make an application would have to undergo a site plan review by the Planning Board so here's point that's it in a nutshell I'd welcome it to public comment so open it up to the public in the room and online and again you'll be let's speak on this the subject to us hi good evening Laverne tenenberg Riverhead I would like to state that I believe that the proposed code change before you tonight is misguided and inappropriate Roanoke Avenue is a stately road with a 30 mile per hour speed limit lined with stately homes and bordered by Roanoke Heights development Roanoke Heights homes were built in the 1930s and 1940s before Whether it was a hospital or old country road, and they were placed on relatively small lots, often only 60 feet wide and about a fifth of an acre.
